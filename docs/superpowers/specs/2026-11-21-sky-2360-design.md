# Sky and lighting pass: design (2026-11-21)

**Status:** approved by the owner on 2026-11-21 through short choices. The concept page is
https://claude.ai/artifact/53u3DtmBFBgVEcamADKUeA. This is enhancement D12's sky and lighting part.
**Branch:** `sky-2360` (worktree `E:\NH-skies`). No Studio work happens in this pass, because another session holds NH Combat Test.

## Owner decisions
| Decision | Choice |
|---|---|
| Direction | **A Candlelit Gothic** for dawn, day and dusk (painted skies, cool blue-violet light against candle-gold windows), with **B Moonlit Storybook** nights (brighter and cloudier, teal-violet, readable). |
| Night brightness | "Dark, but not too dark like the current one": nights must keep paths, outfits and the skyline readable. |
| Seasons | Autumn is permanent. **Winter, Spring, Summer and Nightmare Nights** are event seasons only. |
| Sky animation | The full set: drifting Clouds, the night wisp ring, storm flashes, and a slow sky turn (only if Studio shows no seam). |

## Research that shaped it
DevForum (Nov 2026) and two videos. Full notes are in the side-session notes, `sky-lighting-research.md`.
- **Draw distance:** the minimum is now 500 studs at QL1 (800 at QL2, 1200 at QL3, 1600 at QL4). Don't design around exact numbers.
- **Quality-level reads:** reading `UserGameSettings.SavedQualityLevel` to switch effects is discouraged and planned for deprecation. Our fallbacks use the game's own Effects Quality setting and frame time instead.
- **Performance Simulator** (Device Simulator > Performance): Q1-Q21 plus RAM tiers, in Edit and Play. It's the test bench.
- **Unified Lighting:** use `LightingStyle = Realistic` with `PrioritizeLightingQuality = true`, so the engine drops draw distance before lighting.
- **Bug:** the Atmosphere ignores `Sky.SkyboxOrientation` (Aug 2026), so a turned sky can leave a seam. Dawn therefore gets its own painted sky, and the slow sky turn is gated on a Studio check.
- **PS5:** players report nights much darker on PS5 than on PC. Check every night on a console profile.
- **Sky faces:** uploads cap at 1024, so faces are 1024 PNGs painted from 3D directions (no seams). Swapping skies is cheap, because the cubemap regenerates in the background.
- **Videos:** night is not pitch black. Raise and blue the ambient, set ColorShift_Top to blue moonlight, add a little EnvironmentDiffuse, lower the contrast slightly, and add a bit more atmosphere density. Light pools come from stacked near and far PointLights.

## 1. Sky sets (19 sets, 114 faces at 1024 PNG)
| Season | Sets | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Autumn (permanent) | `Dawn`, `Day`, `Dusk`, `Night` | Dawn is painted on its own (rose-gold, sun at +X); no more turned dusk faces. Night is B style: an ink-blue zenith, a lit horizon band, and moonlit cloud banks low in the sky (the moon's path stays clear). |
| Moon nights | `NightNew`, `NightCrimson`, `NightBlue` | Full, waxing and waning moons use `Night`. New moon: the deepest sky but still readable, with more stars through `StarCount`. Crimson: blood-dark cloud undersides with a red rim. Blue: the existing aurora idea, repainted to match. |
| Weather | `Storm` | Repainted to match the new style. |
| Winter event | `WinterDay`, `WinterDusk`, `WinterNight` | A snow-light overcast, frosted cloud colours, and a cold, bright snow night. |
| Spring event | `SpringDay`, `SpringDusk`, `SpringNight` | Soft green-gold days, pink dusk, a misty night. |
| Summer event | `SummerDay`, `SummerDusk`, `SummerNight` | High warm days, a long amber dusk, a short bright night. |
| Nightmare Nights event | `NightmareDusk`, `NightmareNight` | A blood-red dusk, then a crimson night with ember-lit clouds. |

Every face is `NHSky<Set>_<Face>.png` in `art/group-upload-skies/`, painted by `tools/vfx/make_skyboxes.py`. The new sets are added to its `SKIES` table, and the existing Day and Dusk stay. The painter keeps its rules:
- the moon and stars are left out, because Roblox draws them;
- the upper sky along the moon's path (rising at +X, crossing the -Z side) stays clean;
- the house palette applies.

The preview sheet and the seam report are re-run for every set.

## 3. Moon and event modifiers
Modifiers apply on top of the blended key, weighted by "how much night it is" (0 at 18.5, 1 from 20, the reverse at dawn).
- **Full moon:**
  - Brightness ×1.25 and Exposure +0.05;
  - the moon still swells to 18 (World.FullMoon), and the werewolf effects are unchanged.
- **Waxing and waning:** no change; they are the base night.
- **New moon:**
  - sky `NightNew`; Brightness ×0.4;
  - Ambient +6,6,12 to make up for the missing moon, so it stays readable;
  - Saturation -0.12 and tint 210,216,255;
  - StarCount 5000 (owned by World.MoonPhases).
- **Crimson moon:**
  - sky `NightCrimson`;
  - OutdoorAmbient 130,70,84 and ColorShift_Top 255,120,120;
  - Atmos Color 150,70,80 and Decay 70,20,30;
  - Clouds Color 180,80,90;
  - grade tint 255,214,214, contrast +0.04.
- **Blue moon:**
  - sky `NightBlue`;
  - OutdoorAmbient 90,130,190 and ColorShift_Top 160,210,255;
  - Atmos Color 90,130,180;
  - grade tint 210,232,255.
- **Events** set a workspace attribute, `NHSeason` = `Winter|Spring|Summer|Nightmare` (nil = Autumn). Each event:
  - overrides the sky names for Day, Dusk and Night;
  - layers partial key overrides by key name.

## 4. Sky motion (new `Controllers.World.SkyMotion`)
| Layer | Behaviour | High | Medium | Low |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clouds | `workspace.GlobalWind` sets the drift (slow; storm 3x). Cover, Density and Color come from the key. | on | on | on, half the wind |
| Wisp ring | 3 curved Beams with `NHWisp_A/B` textures (1024×256, horizontally tiling, alpha). They hang between Attachments on a client-only anchor part that follows the camera (`CanCollide`, `CanQuery`, `CastShadow` off, `Transparency` 1). The engine scrolls them on the GPU (`TextureSpeed` 0.01-0.03, no per-frame script), and Beams no longer drop segments at low QL (Nov 2026). Their bands cross the moon's path. `LightInfluence` 0; the colour and transparency come from the key's cloud colour, fading in at dusk. | 3 beams | 1 beam | off |
| Storm flashes | Atmosphere Glare and Haze plus a grade brightness pulse. 0.08-0.25 s, 2-3 flickers, at most 3 Hz, never white. Reduce Flashing gives one soft pulse. | on | on | on |
| Sky turn | `SkyboxOrientation.Y` +2° per in-game hour, wrapping at the sky swap. | gated | gated | off |

- The tier comes from the game's Effects Quality setting, then is demoted one step while the average frame time stays over 22 ms for 5 s. It never reads `SavedQualityLevel`.
- The sky turn ships only if a Studio test shows no Atmosphere seam at QL21 and QL1.

## 5. Wiring plan (Studio, later)
1. **`Config.DayCycle`:**
   - takes the new keys, the `Skies.Sets` ids (found in group 15981569 after the owner uploads them) and `Seasons`;
   - adds `Moon` modifiers;
   - schedules Dawn as its own set (drop the -133° turn).
2. **`Controllers.World.DayCycle`:**
   - writes the new Lighting fields, Clouds and SunRays;
   - picks the season from `workspace.NHSeason`;
   - applies the moon modifier from `workspace.NHMoonEventTonight` or the phase (Lineage).
3. **One grade:** `World.FullMoon` and `World.MoonPhases` stop writing their own ColorCorrection grades (`NHMoonGrade`, `NHNewMoonGrade`); their numbers become DayCycle modifiers. They keep the moon texture, size, stars, banners and eyes.
4. **Cine `Looks`:** they keep their own grade during cuts. Retune `Night` to match the new night (tint 214,228,240 stays; contrast 0.1 to 0.04).
5. **Night sight:** recheck its grade against the brighter base, so it still reads as a boost.
6. **`Controllers.World.SkyMotion`:** a new controller; it stays out of the main menu, like DayCycle.
7. **Test:** Performance Simulator Q1, Q4, Q10 and Q21, a phone RAM tier, and a console profile at 23:00 and 02:00 on each moon. Also check the Lighting and Atmosphere at the Wickfield hometown (4000,0,4000) and the school.

## Out of scope
- Snow and rain particles: the ambience system owns them.

- Terrain scattering, splines and decals (early access).
