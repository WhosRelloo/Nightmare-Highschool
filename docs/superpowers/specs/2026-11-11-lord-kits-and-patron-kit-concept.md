# Lord kits for every race, and the Sanguine Atelier kit: concept (2026-11-11)

**Status:** concept, v2 (2026-11-11), approved; the Sanguine Atelier is built first. The owner loved v1 and asked for it "a little cooler"; this version adds the living pieces, presence and set moments below. Nothing is built.

**The ask:** "I need a Big kit for Lord Style 3D clothing and accessories for EACH race. I also want you to design a cool Kit for the other Owner. He said he wants his kit to be a noble, classy vampire style, but on some blood art type of stuff. He liked the Art Guild [the Ink Club, the artists' clique], so he wants his own custom stuff for HIS version."

## How 3D clothing works on our R6 characters
- Roblox's layered 3D clothing works only on R15 characters. Ours are R6.
- So each Lord outfit is a 2D Shirt and Pants base (the coat's body, the waistcoat, the trousers) under 3D pieces:
  - rigid accessories: crowns, collars, pauldrons, cuffs, jewellery, held items
  - rigged pieces that swing on the hair and cape sway systems: coat-tails, mantles, trains, hems, chains
- Every piece fits all body types and heights (Body Types and Height v2), with no clipping (the accessory clearance checks).
- **Not repeated:** the accessories kit's race pieces (signet ring, torc, lantern, hood) and the admin regalia (thorn crown, crow mantle). The Lord kits are the grand top tier.

## Who gets a Lord kit
- **Who:** a race's Lord kit unlocks at **Tier III** of that race. The legendary sub-race (Royal Vamp, Alpha, Pope, Crypt Lord, Hollow King, and Superhuman for Humans) unlocks one extra signature piece.
- **Where:** the pieces show in the Wardrobe only for students of that race (server-checked), and they are gender-tagged where needed.
- **Optional:** a Lord aura effect that toggles in Settings, with a calm version for Reduce Motion.

## What makes them cooler (v2)

Every Lord kit gets the same five layers on top of its pieces, each in its race's own style:

1. **A living piece.** One piece reacts to the world: it moves, breathes or changes with the moon phase, the time of day or combat.
2. **Presence.** A Lord walks into a room and the room notices.
   - Nearby candles lean toward them and gutter.
   - Students nearby glance over.
   - A faint ground effect follows each step.
   - Reduce Motion turns all of this off.
3. **The Awakening.** Wearing the full set awakens it. The trims start to glow and the signature effect switches on, so a whole set looks different from mixed pieces.
4. **An arrival.** On spawning, teleporting or entering a fight, the Lord gets a two-second entrance in the kit's style, like the admin crows.
5. **A Lord's pose.** An emote unique to the kit, with its own short effect, for showing off.

Effects stay readable in a fight and never cover other players. On low graphics, each layer falls back to a lighter version.

- **v2, Pose:** "The Oath": the cane-sword is drawn and raised, and a silver sigil of the school crest flares on the ground.

## The other owner's kit: "The Sanguine Atelier"

**The idea:** a noble vampire who is also the Ink Club's master artist, a patron-painter of the night court. It's classy, aristocratic and dark, painted in crimson. It's his alone: only his account can wear it, which the server checks.

**Moderation rule:** Roblox warned us about blood once, so this kit is **crimson paint, ink and lacquer, never blood**.
- The "blood art" look comes from crimson brushstrokes, splatters on canvas and on his gloves and hems, wine-dark velvet, red roses and gilded frames.
- No drips on skin, no wounds, and nothing that reads as real blood.
- Paint splatter stays on cloth, canvas and tools.

**Pieces (about 12)**
1. **Atelier tailcoat:** black velvet (2D base) with crimson brushstroke embroidery climbing from the hem, and swinging 3D tails splashed with crimson paint on the inside.
2. **The palette brooch:** a gilded artist's palette worn at the throat. Its "paints" are rubies, garnets and black onyx.
3. **The Master's brush:** a long gilded brush held like a wand. Its trail leaves crimson brushstrokes in the air that fade. This is the kit's VFX signature.
4. **A painted half-cape:** a dark cape on a blackened-gilt frame bar at the shoulders. Its face is near-black velvet, with an original portrait of a vampire lady painted into it in deep shadow tones: blacks, wine and oxblood, with only the crimson highlights catching the light, like an old master's portrait in a candlelit gallery. **Owner (2026-11-11): the cape must fit the theme and still look dark for him.** It is never a bright canvas; it reads as a dark gothic cape first and a painting second.
5. **A frame crown:** a circlet shaped like an ornate picture frame, with a single crimson stone.
6. **Orbiting miniatures:** three tiny framed paintings that circle slowly at shoulder height.
7. **Painter's gloves:** fine black kid gloves, fingertips stained crimson with paint.
8. **The cravat:** a crimson silk cravat with a gold stickpin shaped like a brush tip.
9. **A brush quiver at the hip:** gilded brushes and a palette knife in a tooled leather holster.
10. **The easel staff:** a tall walking staff topped with a tiny gilded easel holding a glowing canvas.
11. **Rose boutonnière:** a crimson rose that sheds a petal now and then.
12. **His entrance:** a teleport or emote where he paints himself into the scene with three huge brushstrokes. It's his version of the admin crow teleport, and it plays with his kit.

**The Ink Club tie-in:** wearing the kit in the Ink Studio makes the studio's paintings glow faintly crimson.

### v2: what makes it cooler
- **The living portrait:** the vampire lady painted into his dark half-cape is alive. Her eyes follow whoever stands behind him, and she changes her pose at midnight. Under a crimson moon her crimson highlights smoulder a little brighter, but the cape stays dark.
- **Gallery Mode (his signature emote):** a gilded easel unfolds in front of him. He paints a quick crimson portrait of the nearest player in a few bold strokes, then hands it to them as a small framed keepsake: a decoration for their dorm, signed with his sigil. It's social, it's fun, and it's unique to him.
- **The brush trail spells his sigil:** idle flourishes with the Master's brush trace his personal monogram in crimson strokes that hang in the air for a moment.
- **The orbiting miniatures become a halo:** when he stands still, the three tiny paintings slow, rise and line up into a frame-halo behind his head.
- **Presence:** each step leaves a faint crimson brushstroke footprint that fades, and paintings on walls nearby turn their gilt frames toward him slightly.
- **The Awakening:** with the full set on, the palette brooch's gems light up one by one like fresh paint, and the frame crown's stone glows.
- **Arrival, "The Unveiling":** a giant canvas on a gilt frame appears, crimson strokes sweep across it and paint his figure, and then he steps out of the frame as it dissolves into rose petals and ink motes.
- **A "Patron's Seat":** he can call up a velvet-and-gilt chair to sit and admire the room. It's a cosmetic seat that leaves when he stands.
- **Still no blood:** everything stays crimson paint, ink, velvet and roses. Nothing is ever on skin, and nothing drips from a body.

## How it would be made
- **Assets:** modelled and rigged in headless Blender, baked at 2048, and published through the group bulk import.
  - Renders on an R6 body, front, side and back, go to the owners for approval before import.
  - The other owner approves his kit personally.
- **Coats and robes:** each Lord coat or robe has a matching 2D Shirt and Pants base made with the clean-edge clothing pipeline, so there are no black seams.
- **Order (owner-approved 2026-11-11):**
  1. The Sanguine Atelier: first, once the accessories session's kits are finished.
  2. The Vampire Elder: closest to it, so it shares lessons.
  3. Then the Werewolf, Priest, Human, Ghoul and Wraith kits.
- **Roughly 50-55 pieces in all.** They'd be made in batches like the accessories kit, after that kit is finished.
