Nightmare Highschool · HUD concept · direction A

The Bell Tower

A new top-right HUD for the school clock, the notoriety Bells, your conduct record and the tracked quest. All the art on this page was baked for this concept with the game's own PBR shader. The moon icons, map and record parchment are existing art.

The frames are drawn at the game's 1920×1080 design size and scaled to fit your screen. Use 1:1 to see true size. Game text is never below 20 design px.

2 · Vocabulary

What each piece means

Hollow

Something was seen, but nobody knows who did it. Slip away and it clears.

Tarnished

A partial description is going round. The witness notes below the bells show what they know.

Lit

Someone can see you right now. Break line of sight.

Swinging

They've lost sight of you and are sweeping the area. Stay clear, hide, or get to class.

Witness notes

Only what witnesses actually know is drawn. To shed a note: change at a wardrobe or locker, revert your form, stash the weapon, split up. A face can't be shed; it fades over in-game days.

The conduct seal

One greyscale seal, tinted per level in game. Suspension breaks it. It's hidden while you're Clear and owe nothing.

4 · Directions

Three directions, one chosen

A was chosen. B and C are shown at concept level, built from the same new bake, for comparison.

Chosen

A · The Bell Tower

Cast brass and black iron. Five bells under a timetable dial. It ties into the school bell and the existing skull-bell alert.

NOTICEDEast Wing · 9:52 PM

B · The Prefect's Ledger

Paper-led: notices pinned in crimson ink and stamped as they escalate. Calmer, but it reads slowly at a glance mid-chase.

C · The Vigil

Candles that light one by one. Beautiful, but it borrows the Vigil faction's candle, which muddles faction meaning.
