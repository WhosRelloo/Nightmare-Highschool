# Sports 1.0: clubs, shared match core, venues, basketball (implementation plan)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox syntax.

**Goal:** working basketball (pickup and club matches, 1v1 to 5v5, drills) on a sport-agnostic match core, with clubs, coaches, rewards and all three venues built (the pitch and field are built now; soccer and football come in 1.1 and 1.2).

**Architecture:**
- **The server owns the rules and the ball.**
  - A possessed ball is welded to the carrier; every client animates the dribble locally.
  - Releases are resolved by the server: make or miss is rolled from server-timed shot holds, contest, distance and stats, and the ball then flies an aimed ballistic arc that the server owns.
- **Clients send intents** (one rate-limited `NHSports.Action` remote) and draw the HUD, meter and poses.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-30-sports-design.md`

## Global constraints
- NH Combat Test only. Saving goes only through ProfileService (`PlayerData.Sports`, action `SetSports`). Everything server-authoritative.
- Original names and art. UI meets the audit bar (≥18 px, contrast ≥3:1).
- Studio is shared: announce Play to the UI-kit session; Edit installs only.
- Body poses and bone transforms are written on **PreSimulation**.

## Tasks
1. **Data and config.** PlayerData `Sports = {}` and `SetSports`; `Config.Sports` with a pure `Sanitize` and `RankFor`.
   - Tests: sanitize, rank thresholds, perk lookup.
2. **Clubs service.** Join/leave (one club), `AddClubExp`, `Record`, `Club` and `ClubRank` attributes, remotes.
   - Coach dialogue modules (Varga, Mallory, Dunmore): join, leave, rank status, tryouts.
   - Cast entries and NpcPosts.
3. **Progression integration.** Exp keys `SportDrill`, `SportMatch`, `SportWin` (category `Sport`); `SportExp` active; label.
4. **Venues.** Build the gym (court 94×50 studs scaled to R6, hoops at regulation height 10 ft = 10 studs), the pitch and the field.
   - Attributes per the spec, scoreboards (SurfaceGui), team pads (A and B per venue), drill stations, coach posts, compass labels.
   - Overlap and clearance check.
5. **Ball service.** Possess, drop, launch to target (solve the initial velocity for an apex height), pickup scan at 10 Hz, out-of-bounds callback, reset to spawn.
   - Tests: the launch solver hits the target within 0.2 studs (pure math).
6. **Match core.**
   - Pads → countdown → teams → Live; clock; score; halftime; end; rewards (Progression, Yen, Rep, club EXP and record).
   - Downed / left / bounds → removal; join mid-match into open slots; scoreboards; `InSport` attribute; HUD state remote.
   - Sport plug-in API: `Setup(match)`, `Start`, `Tick(dt)`, `OnAction(player, action, args)`, `OnBall(event)`, `HalfCourt`.
7. **Basketball rules.**
   - Server-timed shot meter; make chance from timing, contest, distance, stats and race perk; layups and dunks; 3-pointers.
   - Passes to the teammate faced; steals (cooldown, ankle-break immunity during moves, reach-in); blocks (jump window); rebounds.
   - 24 s shot clock; first to 21 or two 4-minute halves; check ball after a score; half court ≤2v2 with a clear-the-arc rule; out of bounds → the other team inbounds at the top.
   - Drills: Shootaround (10 spots / 60 s), Cone Dribble (course timer).
8. **Client.**
   - HUD bar (team colours, score, game clock, shot clock, stamina) and the shot meter (hold/release, green window from the server state).
   - Input map (click shoot, E pass, Q/R/F/C moves, G post).
   - Dribble bounce for every carrier, procedural poses (shoot, layup, dunk, dribble moves, steal reach, block jump), a post-game card, and a drill timer.
9. **Tryout quests** (one per club) via the new `Drill` / `SportMatch` objectives; club tag in the Students list; crouch disabled in sports.
10. **Audit and playtest.**
    - Pure tests; exploit checks (spammed actions, shooting without the ball, far pickups, launch caps).
    - A code-review agent on every sports module; solo Play of the drills, the queue → 1v1 start, the HUD and post-game.
    - Docs, changelog, Discord post, push, vault.
