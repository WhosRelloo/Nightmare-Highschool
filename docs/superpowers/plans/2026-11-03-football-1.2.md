# Football (Sports 1.2) Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Iron Wardens football on the Field, playing like Football Fusion 2 with an optional quick play call.
**Spec:** [../specs/2026-10-30-sports-design.md](../specs/2026-10-30-sports-design.md), section 5 (revised 2026-11-03).
**Architecture:** a `Services.Sports.Football` plug-in for the shared match core (`Services.Sports.Matches`), the shared `Ball`, a client `Controllers.Gui.Football` built on `Controllers.Gui.Sports`, and a practice area west of the Field for the two drills.

## Global constraints
- NH Combat Test only. Server owns the ball and every outcome; clients send intent (aim point, direction), and the server measures every charge.
- Saving only through `Clubs` (`PlayerData.Sports` via `SetSports`). Never touch Race.
- New UI uses only existing kit textures and components (`Kit.Panel`, `Kit.Bar`, `Kit.Text`, `Theme.Images`).
- Fx names prefixed `F_` (Gui.Sports reacts to unprefixed names).
- No running/sprint, movement or combat VFX changes.

## Field facts (from `workspace.Sports.Field`)
- Goal lines x −154 (west) and −34 (east); 2 studs a yard; width z −190..−126; turf top y 1.3.
- `SportGoal = A` is the west end zone: team A scores there and attacks −X. B attacks +X.
- Goalposts at x −171 / −17, uprights at z −151 / −165 (14 studs apart), crossbar height from the `Crossbar` parts.

## Tasks
1. **Config** (`ReplicatedStorage.Config.Sports`): `Sports.Football` tuning (teams, halves, throw, catch, juke, dive, hit stick, swat, kick, plays and routes), `Drills.RouteRunner` and `Drills.QBTargets`.
2. **Shared core touch-ups** (`Matches`, `Gui.Sports`): per-sport start word and lead word, a `MvpScore` plug-in hook, a per-sport card line, a play-clock label and phase text. Soccer and basketball keep their current text.
3. **Server rules** (`Services.Sports.Football`): Setup, Start, Tick, OnAction, Hud, Busy, OnRemoved, Halftime, Stop and Reset, covering:
   - modes: Kickoff → Huddle → Line → Play → Dead (→ Convert / Kickoff)
   - downs and distance, the rush count wall, the forward-pass rule and handoffs
   - catches, swats, touch tackles, jukes, spins, dives, hit sticks and fumbles
   - kickoffs, onside kicks, punts, field goals, touchbacks, safeties, extra points and 2-point tries
   - a clock that runs only on live plays, penalties, and race perks
4. **Client** (`Controllers.Gui.Football`):
   - input for every key in the spec, with touch and gamepad buttons
   - throw charge and landing marker, kick meter
   - line of scrimmage, first-down line and route drawing; coverage hints
   - down-and-distance HUD and the rush count
   - dashes and stuns, and simple poses
5. **Practice area and drills**: Route Runner (a throwing machine) and QB Targets (moving rings), with prompts on start pads, the drill HUD, and bests saved through `Clubs.DrillBest`.
6. **Hookups**:
   - `Config.Quests.WardensTryout` (Dunmore: Route Runner, then a match)
   - Dunmore's dialogue lines
   - the Gazette club notice
   - TestBridge: `FootballState`, `FootballDummy`, and Football added to the `SportAction` drill list
7. **Test in Play (solo)**: a solo test match, test dummies for tackles, kickoffs, field goals, punts, downs, both drills and the tryout objective. Two-player catches and contests wait for the owner's multi-player test.
8. **Docs**: Sports.md football section, CHANGELOG, a Discord post, Implementation-Status, and the vault.
