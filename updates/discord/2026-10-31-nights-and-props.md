## 🌙 Nightmare Highschool: Real Nights and a Real School

**Nights look like nights now.** From 8 PM the school sinks into a moody blue-violet dark, with pink-gold dawns and amber dusks in between. (Crimson *Nightmare Nights* are coming…)

**The school got real materials.** Worn tiled classroom floors, varnished desks, chipped painted metal, slate and plaster all catch the light.

**New furniture, hand-modelled:**
🪑 gothic wrought-iron student desks with inkwells
📜 oak teacher desks with crimson leather tops
🧑‍🏫 carved chalkboards in every classroom
🔒 crimson lockers with arched doors, padlocks and an actual inside

**Lockers open.** Hide in one and you'll see the doors swing shut behind you. Search locker 113 and watch the note swing with it.

**Comfort settings work everywhere:** Camera Shake at 0% means no shake anywhere, and Reduce Flashing calms the lightning on the menu.

Coming next: chairs, a staff-room lounge, a vending machine you can thump, an altar, and a bookshelf that… doesn't stay full. 📚
