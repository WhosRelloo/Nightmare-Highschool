## 🪞 Nightmare Highschool: Customization & Main Menu Fixes

We went through character customization and the main menu from top to bottom: every option, every save, every button. Here's what's better:

**🧑‍🎓 Your student looks right everywhere.** The students posed in the main menu now keep their hair, and they show your bloodline's skin and uniform.
**🎭 Faces stay put.** Painted eyes, brows and mouths no longer vanish on avatar heads.
**💇 Hair behaves.** No more doubled hair at spawn, and no more going bald when Roblox's catalog hiccups.
**👔 The Wardrobe never overwrites your look** with defaults while it's still loading.
**📜 Names are safe.** A slow name check can no longer undo a spin you just bought or unlock your enrolled name.
**⚙️ Settings behaves in the menu.** The menu no longer reacts to keys pressed in Settings, so you can't enter school by accident.
**🩸 The Bloodline tab** gets its own camera angle and shows your new roll right away.
**🎮 Gamepad:** turn your student with the right stick in the creator.

Plus a handful of fixes so a bad connection never leaves you stuck on a black screen.
