## 🩸 Nightmare Highschool: Bloodlines

**🐺 Three new races**
• **Werewolves, Ghouls and Wraiths** now walk the halls next to Humans, Priests and Vampires.
• Your race is still fate. You roll it, you never pick it. Each race has rare bloodlines like **Bloodmoon**, **Crypt Lord** and **Hollow King**.

**⚔️ Every race plays differently**
• **Vampires** hit harder at night and heal on every kill, but weaken by day.
• **Werewolves** are tough to put down, and the **full moon** makes them stronger.
• **Ghouls** shrug off hits and mend by feeding.
• **Wraiths** are hard to spot and hard to stagger.
• **Priests** burn the undead. **Humans** learn faster, and their Slayers hunt monsters.
• Rarer bloodlines get stronger perks. Every race has a weakness too.

**📜 The Bloodline tab**
• See your race, your tier, your perk, your weakness and what your next tier brings. It's in the character creator and in your Student Record.
• Vampires keep pale or dark skin. Ghouls wear ghoul tones.

**🕯️ Crouch**
• Hold **Ctrl** to crouch and creep. You're much harder to notice while sneaking.
• **Ctrl** while sprinting slides.
