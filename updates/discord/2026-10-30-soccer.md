## ⚽ Nightmare Highschool: The Grave Rovers Take the Pitch

**Soccer is here.** Coach Mallory's Grave Rovers play on the walled west pitch, under the floodlights and the fog.

**🦶 Shoot:** hold click to charge and release. Full power hits hard, and holding **A/D** bends it late.
**🎯 Pass, dribble, feint:** **R** passes to whoever you're looking at, **Q** bursts past a defender (tackle into it and your ankles break), and **right-click** feints.
**🛡️ Defend:** **E** slide-tackles. Miss it and you're on the grass. Click as the ball comes in to volley or head it.
**🧤 Keepers:** at 3v3 and up, take the keeper pad: long reach, diving saves, throws.
**🩸 Bloodrush (T):** steals, ankle breaks and goals build it. Unleash it for harder shots and a burst of speed, with an aura in your bloodline's colour.

**🏆 First to 10**, a 5-goal lead, or golden-goal overtime. Ranked club games after school.
**🥅 Drills:** Target Practice and Wall Passes, on the practice ground south of the pitch.
**📜 Grave Rovers Tryout:** prove yourself to Coach Mallory.
