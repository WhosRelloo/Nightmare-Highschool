## 🔍 Nightmare Highschool: UI Clean-Up

We went through **every screen** in the game, from the loading screen and menu to the creator, the HUD and every panel, and checked what's hard to read.

**👓 Easier to read**
• Small text is bigger: the EXP level, your Student Record, the Students list, the compass, Name & Clan and the Spin Shop.
• Faint grey notes and menu hints are brighter.

**🗂️ Layers fixed**
• The EXP line no longer cuts across your Journal, Factions and Settings panels.

**☀️ Daylight**
• The compass and top bar stay dark and on-theme under a bright sky. No more navy blue.

**🧭 Coming next**
• Laptop-wide text scaling, phone layouts and a gamepad pass.
