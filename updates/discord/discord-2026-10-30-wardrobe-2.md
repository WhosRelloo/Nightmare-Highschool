## 🩸 Wardrobe 2.0: faces, hair and your bloodline
Press **K** to open the Wardrobe. It now has five tabs.

👁️ **Eyes:** 8 hand-drawn shapes (Sharp, Hooded, Fierce, Doe, Sleepless, Cat, Gentle, Calm), each in a **male** or **female** version, in any colour you like with the colour wheel or a HEX code.
🎭 **Brows & mouths** are their own pieces now. Mix any eyes with 11 brow styles (including thick Bold, Heavy and Bushy) and 20 anime mouths, from smiles and shouts to pouts, scowls and grimaces, plus Cat :3, Fang and Shark (with fuller lips if you like). Faces now stay razor-sharp on every graphics setting. Brows match your hair colour, or pick your own.
🌕 **Transformations change your face:**
- **Vampire:** blade-slit crimson eyes and fangs
- **Wolf:** ragged golden eyes and a snarl
- **Ghoul:** milky eyes, sunken sockets and spreading veins
- **Wraith:** a blank, glowing stare

Go deeper and vampire and ghoul eyes turn **jet black**. The veins take on your own skin tone.
💇 **Hair:** use a built style, or paste **any Roblox hair ID**. Colour it with the wheel or a HEX code.
👔 **Uniform:** the official Red Vest, Red Shirt, Black Vest and Black Shirt, with the school trousers.
🎰 **Lineage:** your race is decided by fate. **Spin** for Human, Priest or Vampire, then hope for a rare variant: Superhuman, Grand Priest, *Pope*, Nobleblood or *Royal Vamp*. You also roll **2 traits**, from Night Owl up to the legendary *Fated*.

*You start with a free roll, then a few rerolls. Choose wisely...*
