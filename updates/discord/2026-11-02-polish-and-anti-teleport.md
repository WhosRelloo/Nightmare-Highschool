# Polish pass and an anti-teleport check (1.9.3)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-02**

The last batch of the effects roadmap is in, plus a fix that keeps teleport cheaters honest.

**Feels better**
- Rings and markers are soft and glowing now, not hard neon discs: goals, dunks, swishes and the soccer landing marker.
- Hit a swish and embers burst through the net. Hit a three and gold shards come with them.
- Sports result cards pop in, your rewards count up, and a personal best gets stamped.
- Every EXP gain sends a few gold sparks along your bar.
- When you learn someone's name, it writes itself in over their head.

**School life**
- Take your seat in class and a small desk bell rings.
- Get written up and the new level is stamped across your screen in red ink. Clean your record up and you'll see a gold "Record improved".
- Night Study is candle-lit: warm, flickering edges for the whole lesson.

**Fair play**
- The server now notices when someone jumps across the map or flies straight up, and puts them back. Everything the game does itself still works: entering school, duels, sports set pieces, admin tools.
