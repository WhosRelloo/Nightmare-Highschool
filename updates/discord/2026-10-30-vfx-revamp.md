## 🕯️ Nightmare Highschool: The Visual Revamp Begins

We're starting a full revamp of everything you *see* outside of combat: the school, night, quests, the supernatural, rewards and menus. The plan is done, and here are the first fixes:

**👻 Wraiths flicker properly.** The Tier III Wraith ascension now flickers like a fading ghost, instead of getting stuck half see-through.
**📈 Level-ups feel right.** The EXP bar fills up, stamps your new level and starts fresh. No more sliding backwards.
**🎥 Your camera stays yours.** Entering school keeps your field-of-view setting.
**📦 Your stuff is yours.** Dropped quest items and detention trash only show for you, so no more mystery glowing boxes.
**🚪 Lockers behave.** No more lockers stuck on "Occupied".
**⚽ Goals look like goals.** The goal burst now erupts out of the net.
**♿ New comfort settings:** Reduce Motion, Reduce Flashing and High-quality Effects.

Coming next: real nights (moody blue-violet), a school bell, hall monitors who actually warn you, and quest moments worth seeing. And later… **Nightmare Nights.** 🩸
