## 👊 Nightmare Highschool: Fists and Enrollment

**Fists are here.** Pick up the brawler style: a four-hit combo, a running punch, a downslam and a heavy crit.
**Z** Question Mark Kick: launch them
**X** Body Slam: grab and slam
**C** Bear Hug: crush them (it goes through a block)

**Your race has a finisher.** Stand over a downed body and press **N**:
🩸 **Vampire:** Blood Drain
💀 **Ghoul** and 🐺 **Werewolf:** Eat Body
❄️ **Wraith:** Soul Siphon
✝️ **Human** and **Priest:** Exorcise (only the unholy)
It heals you and feeds your hunger.

**New students start in their own clothes.** Head to the **Enrollment desk** outside the Records Office and pick your uniform. More tops are at the **School Store** (the vending machine), with a preview of you wearing them.

**Wardrobes live in the locker rooms now.** Walk up and press E to change.

Also: you're slower while you're in combat, sliding while holding Shift keeps you sprinting, and capes finally sit right on your shoulders.
