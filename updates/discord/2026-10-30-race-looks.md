## 🦇 Nightmare Highschool: Race Looks

Your bloodline finally shows.

**🧛 Vampires:** sharp ears that grow longer and grander as you evolve, in your own pale or dark skin.
**🐺 Werewolves:** wolf ears and a **tail that moves**. It sways when you stand still, swings when you turn, lifts when you run. Six fur colours, and silver-tipped **Moon-marked** ears and tail at the top tier.
**🧟 Ghouls:** your school uniform comes back torn, stitched and grave-stained. Evolve for stitched straps and a tattered grave shroud.
**👻 Wraiths:** a cold breath and thin wisps that curl off you, faint by day, clearer at night. Frost-tipped fingers for the oldest.

Pick your looks in the **Bloodline** tab. The best ones unlock as your bloodline evolves.
