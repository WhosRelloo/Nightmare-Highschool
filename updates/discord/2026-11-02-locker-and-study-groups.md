# The Locker, snacks and study groups (1.10.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-02**

**The school keeps its own timetable**
- Students walk in before the bell, take their seats in class, fill the halls between periods, and crowd the snack stand at lunch.
- After school they head off to their clubs, and by evening they've gone home.

**Mrs. Okada's concession stand**
- There's a new snack kiosk by the west doors.
- Food heals you over a few seconds: onigiri, melon bread, yakisoba buns, and a Night Bento for the big fights.
- Drinks give a small buff: Ramune steadies your guard, barley tea keeps you quiet, coffee helps in class, and Red Lantern... the teachers pretend not to see.

**Your Locker (G)**
- See everything you carry: your gear on your character, what it adds up to, and every snack.
- Put up to four snacks in your pouch and they go on your hotbar. Click to eat.

**Study groups (U)**
- Team up with up to three classmates.
- Fight near each other for +10% EXP, and your group shares a slice of every fight's EXP.

**Report Card (L)**
- Your level, your stats with one-click upgrades, everything your race, clan, traits, gear and snacks give you, and your school record, all in one place.

**Fixed**
- Gear chests no longer lie on their side.
