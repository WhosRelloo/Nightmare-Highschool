## 🥊 Fluid fights

👊 **Fists and Claws hit like they should.** Running attacks always connect now. Your lunge stops right in front of your target instead of carrying you past them.
💥 **Downslams land from any height.** Jump, hang for a beat, then dive, timed so you hit the ground on the impact frame. If someone's close in front of you, the dive curves to meet them.
🩸 **Claws hits slash:** every Claws hit and block now has its own effect and sound.

🗡️ **Claws students start with their claws on.** No need to equip.
💨 **Dash, then keep running.** Hold sprint through a dash (forward, back or sideways) and you go straight back into a full-speed run.
🏃 **No more lost sprints:** pressing dash while it's on cooldown no longer stops your run.

🚪 **Back to the main menu:** open Settings (Menu or M) and press **Main Menu**, then press it again to confirm. Your progress is saved and you can pick any save slot. You can't use it in the middle of a fight.
