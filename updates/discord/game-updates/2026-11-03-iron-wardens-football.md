# Kickoff: Iron Wardens football (1.14.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-03**

The north field finally has a game on it. Football plays fast and loose, like the football games you already know on Roblox.

**How a down works**
- Huddle up: the QB can call a play with **1-6** and your route lights up on the grass. Or skip it and just play.
- The QB snaps with **Space**. The defence counts **three Mississippi** before it can rush.
- Aim with your mouse, **hold click** and let go to throw. A gold ring shows where it'll land. Under-throw a deep ball and it dies short.

**Catching**
- Click to reach and your arms follow the ball. Whoever's hands it touches first comes down with it, so fight for position.
- **Q** dives, **Space** goes up for it. Defenders can pick it off.

**With the ball:** **E / R** juke, **F** spin, **K** kneel.
**On defence:** touch the carrier to tackle, **R** for a hit stick (it can knock the ball loose, but whiff and you eat turf), **C** to swat.

**Kicks:** kickoffs, onside kicks, punts and field goals on a kick meter. Touchdowns are 6, then go for 1 or for 2.

**Practice lanes west of the field**
- **Route Runner:** the machine calls a route and throws to you on the run.
- **QB Targets:** thread passes through moving rings for 1, 2 or 3 points.
