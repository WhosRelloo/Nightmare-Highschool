## 🌒 Nightmare Highschool: Nightfall

**Tier-ups are brand new.** Every race gets its own cinematic, with a glowing sigil, a pillar of light and a race set piece:
🩸 **Vampire:** a blood pool spreads, bats swarm, blood crystals erupt
🐺 **Werewolf:** the floor cracks, rock flies, a giant moon rises behind you
💀 **Ghoul:** graves heave open, bone spurs claw out of the earth
❄️ **Wraith:** ice creeps across the floor, frost falls, ice spikes burst up
🗡️ **Human:** a hunter's sigil forges behind you, silver stakes slam down
✝️ **Priest:** a golden cross rises, a halo settles, feathers fall

**Sneaking got tense.** Watchers show a **?** as they start to notice you and a red **!** when you're about to be caught. Get caught and you're grabbed and **THROWN OUT**. At night, the custodian carries a torch.

**Combat tag:** hit or get hit and you're in **DANGER** for 30 s. Changed your mind? Type **mb all** to call it off. 🤝

**Capes** are in the Spin Shop: Vamp, Rogue and Fur. They sway as you run.

Also: **Shift** to sprint, **Left Alt** for shift lock, a Female body option, seals over NPCs who have quests for you, and votive candles you actually light.
