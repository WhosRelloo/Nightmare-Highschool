# Standing Paths (2.31.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-18**

- **You're becoming somebody.** Five Standing Paths now track who you are at school: Academic, Social, Combat, Athletic and Supernatural.
- **Climb by playing.** Pass classes and keep your attendance up, make friends and join a faction, win fights and duels, rise in your sports club, or go looking for what the school hides. Each path has four or five tiers, from Freshman Nobody to Academic Elite, or from Beginner to Feared.
- **Tiers never drop.** Once you've earned one, it's yours. A bad week can't take it back.
- **Every tier gives you its title,** with a banner the moment you earn it.
- **Big moments get announced.** Reach tier 4 and the PA congratulates you by name, or the Midnight Broadcast gives you a shout-out after dark.
- **People notice.** Students greet you by your reputation ("That's the honor student."), and Ms. Sallow, Mr. Pryce, Dr. Whitlock, Ezra and Vesper all have something to say once you've made a name on their path.
