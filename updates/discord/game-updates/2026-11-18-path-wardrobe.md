# Path wardrobe & the Today card (2.34.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-18**

- **Wear your reputation.** Climbing a Standing Path now earns you something to wear. None of it is sold.
- **Academic Pin:** a gilt brooch with an open book and quill. Become an Honor Student.
- **Scarred Knuckle Wraps:** worn fighter's tape on both hands. Become Notorious.
- **Silver Sigil:** a moon-and-star pendant with a softly glowing moonstone. Become a Keeper of Secrets.
- **Three new uniform looks:** Rolled Sleeves (Popular), Loosened Tie (Enforcer) and a well-travelled Worn Blazer (Initiated). The Varsity Jacket now comes with Varsity too.
- **Already there?** If you reached a tier before today, its reward is waiting in your wardrobe.
- **The Today card:** open your phone for a quiet summary of what's on now, the latest rumour, your next path step and your quest lead. Tap a line to jump to it.
- **Phase 3 is complete:** Standing Paths, the Permanent Record, Honours, Trophies and now the path wardrobe.
