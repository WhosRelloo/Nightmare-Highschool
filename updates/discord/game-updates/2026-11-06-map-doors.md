# Behind the scenes: the doors open (1.59.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-06**

- **The new map's doors work now.** Walk up to a door and press E: the leaves swing away from you, and the door closes itself behind you a few seconds later.
- That covers every door from our door kit on the map: the gothic double doors, classroom and headmaster doors, the bookcase door, the iron gate, the sliding iron door and the cellar hatch.
