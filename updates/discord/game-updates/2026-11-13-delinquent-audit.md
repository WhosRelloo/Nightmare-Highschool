# Delinquents, detention and the hall monitor: fixes (2.7.3)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **No more detention from the dressing room.** Time in the character creator no longer counts as skipping class.
  You're only expected in class once you walk into school.
- **Suspended means suspended.**
  - Your HUD tells you to stay out of class instead of sending you to one.
  - Fights and trouble while you're suspended still count when you come back.
- **The hall monitor stays on task.** When it's on its way to break up a fight, it doesn't turn to chase someone else
  halfway there.
- **Bruno's Guard Duty waits for you.** The Ordinary come to prank the Back Lot once you're there to stop them.
- **The Back Lot Toughs won't jump in to fight their own.**
- **Fight rewards end when the fight does.**
