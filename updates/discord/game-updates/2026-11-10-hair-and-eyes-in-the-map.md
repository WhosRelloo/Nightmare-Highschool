# Living hair and blinking eyes in the Map place (1.67.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-10**

- **Hair moves in the Map now.** Every barber cut sways as you walk, turn and fall, and drifts in the wind. The roots stay on your head.
- **Eyes blink.** Painted eyes blink every few seconds and narrow to a squint when you're in a fight.
- **Try it:** press **K**, open the **Hair** tab and pick a cut from the Barber's book (long ones like Wolfcut Ponytail show it best), then walk around.
- **Fixed:** "Open" no longer pops up in archways. Only real doors show it, and they still open with **E**.
