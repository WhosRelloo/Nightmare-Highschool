# The school starts to sound alive (2.17.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **Rooms sound like rooms.** Footsteps, fists and voices now echo the way the space would. Stone corridors ring, the
  chapel carries, the gym booms, and the boiler yard clangs like a pipe.
- **The world around you.** Clocks tick, radiators hum and candles crackle as you walk past. Lockers slam down the
  hall, birds call outside by day, and an owl and a far-off dog answer at night. Every sound was made by us, from
  scratch.
- **Fights come first.** When a fight starts, the background sound steps back so you can hear every swing. In
  cutscenes and conversations the close sounds quiet down and the room stays.
