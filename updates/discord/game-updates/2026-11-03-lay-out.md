# Lay out (1.14.2)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-03**

**A real dive**
- **Q** is a full layout now: you launch the way you're running, carry your speed through the air, and can steer a little before you land.
- Dive from a jump to go up and get high balls.
- You're back on your feet faster, and it costs less stamina.
- You still have to **click** to catch or pick it off mid-dive, and diving at the right moment is how you win a contested ball.
- Ball carriers can dive for the extra yards, or into the end zone.
