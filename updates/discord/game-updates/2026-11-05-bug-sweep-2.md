# The bug sweep, part two (1.55.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

We went back through everything the big sweep found and fixed the rest: well over a hundred smaller bugs. The ones you'll feel:

- **Fair fights:** grabs can't catch you mid-dodge or mid-parry. Nobody can jump into a duel or hit someone sitting a class minigame. Stuns can't wear off early, and an interrupted heavy still goes on cooldown.
- **Chain slides work:** slide right after a slide-jump for the speed boost.
- **Class:** fast presses in Seance Sequence count. Your lesson grade always reaches your report card, even when the bell rings mid-game.
- **Phone:** the wallet's "new money" badge is right again, replying to a text always hits the right one, and arcade scoreboards are fair.
- **Around school:** vending machines and bookshelves keep their prompts, open doors don't block students, skateboard crashes behave, and Lost & Found items drop if you're knocked down.
- **Sports:** no snatching a basketball off the shooter's hand, and football jukes and fumbles play out properly.
- Plus many fixes to the menus, clan spins (the pity now lands on spin 41, as promised), cliques, hall monitors and more.
