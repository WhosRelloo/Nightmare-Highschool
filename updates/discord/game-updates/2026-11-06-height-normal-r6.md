# Height tune-up (1.63.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-06**

- **Average is a normal Roblox R6 now.** A 5'9" student is exactly the size of a standard R6 character, and every height goes up and down from there.
- **Everyone is a little bigger.** Students, and the NPC students around the school, grew about 10% to match. Your rolled height, its rarity and your race's range don't change.
