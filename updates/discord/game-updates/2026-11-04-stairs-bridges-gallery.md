# Stairs, bridges and a gallery (1.38.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

Two more kits are ready for the builders.

**Stairs**
- Worn castle-stone flights with carved gothic arches and pinnacle posts, with or without a cast-iron railing.
- A grand staircase with a crimson runner and gas lanterns, a spiral tower stair, an open iron stair, and a carved landing to join flights into turns.

**Bridges**
- A humped stone footbridge with a pierced gothic parapet and lanterns.
- A Victorian iron footbridge in crimson lattice.
- Straight spans and tall piers to build your own crossings.

You can walk on all of them, and the lanterns light up at dusk.

**The gallery**
- Six original paintings in gilt frames: a moonlit abbey, a wolf at a blood moon, a ghost ship in a storm, a raven on a candlelit skull, a Victorian silhouette and a golden triptych of the school's crest.
- Velvet-rope stanchions, a stack of canvases under a drape and a painter's table with a flickering candle.
