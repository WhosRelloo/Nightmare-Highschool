# Made for phones (1.30.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

Last update put buttons on your phone. This one makes the rest of the screen fit it.

**Everything fits**
On a phone, the menus, the HUD and the main menu are sized for the screen now. Before, they were desktop-sized and ran off the edges.

**Menus that fit**
The Journal and the in-game phone fit on a phone screen at full text size, and their lists scroll. The buttons get out of the way while a menu is open.

**A tidier screen**
The compass, your quest and the Factions and Journal buttons line up on the left, the clock is smaller, your Yen sits on top of the health bar, and the health bar no longer covers your study group.

Tablets keep the bigger layout.
