## 🩸 Nightmare Highschool: Names, Clans & a New Way In

**📜 Sign the register**
• Every student now has a full name: your first name + your **clan**.
• You pick the first name. The clan is your family name, and fate rolls it.
• Your name shows over your head and on your save slot.

**🎲 28 clans, 6 rarities**
• Common to **Legendary**, plus two **Cursed** names the school would rather forget.
• Each clan has its own lore and a perk (perks switch on with the stat system).
• Rare rolls flare the crimson ring in the clan's colour.
• **Pity:** 40 spins in a row without Epic or better means the next one is Epic+.
• New students get a free roll plus 3 spins.

**🛒 Spin Shop**
• Clan, race, variant and trait spins, plus a Name Change, right in the character editor.
• Spins go to the student you're playing.
*(Not on sale yet while we finish testing.)*

**🗂️ New loading screen**
• Loading is now your admission file being processed, over a live shot of the academy at night.
• An asset counter tells you exactly how much is left, and how long.
• When it's done, your file gets stamped **ADMITTED**.

**🕯️ Cleaner screen**
• A slim custom top bar replaces Roblox's buttons: Menu, Chat and Students.
• It fades away when you're not using it.

**✨ Polish**
• Smoother, higher-quality energy around the clock seal.
• The far side of the courtyard is filled in: a west hall, more dead oaks, the fence line and the town lights.

*Your name is in the register now. Try not to make it famous for the wrong reasons.*
