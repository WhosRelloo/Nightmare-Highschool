# The school entrance kit (1.51.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

15 new pieces for the school's front steps, carved from the same weathered castle stone as the stairs, with the same gothic ironwork and gas lanterns.

- **The Moonrise entrance:** climb from the new moon to the full. Every step has a gilt moon phase inlaid in it, and ravens perch on gilt crescents at the foot. The porch has a great moon dial set in its floor. The doorway is a gothic arch lettered *Per Noctem ad Lucem*, under a full-moon rose window that glows at night.
- **Grand and bowed flights:** one under a pierced stone parapet, one with bowed steps that flare wide at the foot.
- **Split and horseshoe stairs:** flights that climb along the front from both sides, or sweep in on curves to meet the porch.
- **Round steps:** a half-circle fan, a moonlit version with phases round every step, a quarter fan for corners, and a round dais for statues or fountains.
- **Porches and lantern newels** to finish them off.

Also fixed: the library's floor globe now shows its map crisply, and its stand is no longer streaky.
