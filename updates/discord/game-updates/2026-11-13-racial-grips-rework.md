# Racial grips, reworked (2.6.2)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **Scarier finishers.** Every racial grip got heavier and more menacing:
  - Vampires creep in with their heads cocked and hands curled like claws.
  - Hunters drive the stake home with their whole weight.
  - Werewolves snarl with their claws out before they pounce.
  - Priests stay bowed over the emptied body after the last word.
- **Kneels look right.** Hunters, priests and vampires now drop into the same crouch you use in game, knee to the
  floor, instead of an awkward lunge. Feet stay planted the whole time.
- **Smoother motion.** Finishers flow from beat to beat, with no snapping, stiff legs or limbs through the floor.
- **Victims fight harder.** A hunter's prey strains up off the floor against the stake. A possessed body twists and
  grabs at the priest's wrist.
