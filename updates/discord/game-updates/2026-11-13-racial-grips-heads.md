# Racial grips: eyes on the prey (2.7.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **Heads follow the kill.** Every finisher now looks at what it's doing:
  - Werewolves bury their heads in the body as they maul, snap at the throat between swipes, and shake their prey
    by the neck before the howl.
  - Hunters watch the heart as they drive the stake.
  - Priests stare down the possessed.
  - Vampires lock onto the neck.
  - Wraiths watch the soul rise.
- **Victims watch you back.** Between the blows they stare at their attacker, fight the stake arm with their eyes on
  it, or glare up at the priest.
