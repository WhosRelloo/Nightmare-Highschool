# Hall furniture (1.13.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-03**

The furniture we teased last update is in the school now.

**In the main hall**
- A grandfather clock stands by Chemistry (Room 103), and its pendulum swings.
- Iron wall sconces hang beside every classroom door. Their flames flicker after dark.
- Hall benches and notice boards line the walls.
- A trophy cabinet stands by History (Room 105).
- The old school bell hangs high above the English corridor (Room 104).

**In the Archive**

- A celestial globe beside the shelves.

Take a walk at night: the halls feel very different by sconce light.
