# New dots, a trophy case, and no more wasted buffs (2.4.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **"Replace your buff?"** Drink something new while another drink's buff still has over a minute left and you'll get
  asked first. Use it again to swap, or don't, and keep what you had.
- **New things get a dot.** Snacks and trophies you haven't looked at in your Locker get a small gold dot. Pick them and
  it's gone.
- **The trophy case.** Filter your Locker by Trophies to see all five beast trophies. The ones you haven't found yet are
  dark shapes with a "?", telling you which beast drops them.
