# Fists get a real moveset (2.10.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **Finish your string your way.** Hit M2 right after a punch lands:
  - after the 1st punch, a **Shove** that sends them flying back;
  - after the 2nd, a **Launcher** into the air;
  - after the 3rd, a **Body Blow** that doubles them over, and wrecks a guard.
- **The Clinch.** Hold block and click to grab and drive a knee into someone turtling. It goes straight through a
  guard, but you'll eat a punch if they're already swinging.
- **Riposte.** Land a parry and hit back fast: your next punch hits harder and staggers them.
- **Smoother clicking.** One click is always exactly one punch now, however fast you click.
