# The map catches up (2.17.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

Everything from today is now on the real school map:
- **Fights draw a crowd.** Students form a ring, chant, bet, and hold their phones up.
- **Tag in your study group** with G, and enemy students now fight with their fists, some with brass knuckles.
- **Painted skies** change with the time of day.
- **Hunger.** Every race gets hungry, and your race's vessel shows how full you are.
- **The school sounds alive.** Rooms echo, and the halls and grounds have their own sounds.
- **Inventory upgrades:** loadouts, "new" dots, the trophy case, and the taste book.
