# The new furniture is out (1.18.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-03**

The forty-two pieces from the last update are now standing in the game world, ready for the builders to place around the school.

- The boiler's furnace door swings open on the fire.
- The valve wheel turns, the filing cabinet's drawer slides out, and the gramophone's record spins when it plays.
- The club and faction banners sway on their rods.
- The football throwing machine's wheels spin, and the chandelier sways.

You'll meet them in the clubrooms, the boiler room, the courtyard and the halls as they're placed.
