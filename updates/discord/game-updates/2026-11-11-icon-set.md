# The icon set (1.84.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-11**

- **No more one seal for everything.** The wax seal used to stand in for nearly every icon in the game. It now has one job, the rarity gem on your items, and everything else has its own icon.
- **47 new icons**, all hand-made for Nightmare High in brass, black iron, crimson enamel, parchment, wax and bone.
- **Notifications** show what kind they are at a glance: a blue "i" for news, a green shield for good news, a gilt warning triangle, a crimson cross, and a star rosette for milestones.
- **Banners** get their own art: a laurel wreath with your new level in it, a wrapped parcel when a purchase lands, bone wings when you evolve, a scroll for every quest step (new, next objective, complete, failed), the full, new, crimson and blue moons, and the ledger when your discipline record improves.
- **Over NPCs' heads:** a crimson shield with "!" for a quest to start, a tick to hand one in, a return arrow to retry, and an hourglass while you're still on it.
- **Around the school:** the school gates stamp in when you arrive, a pennant marks a new place discovered, and a pin shows your waypoint.
- **Sports on touch screens:** every move gets its own button (shoot, pass, euro step, kick, tackle, feint, throw, juke, snap and the rest), coloured by sport.
- **On your phone:** a new wax grade seal on the report card, a struck gold yen for your allowance, new card backs in Memory Match, a cracked pocket watch in Hex Slash and a burning sun rune in Crypt Crush.
