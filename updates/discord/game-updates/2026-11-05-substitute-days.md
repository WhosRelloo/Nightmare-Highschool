# Substitute days (1.47.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

- **Your teacher's out. Someone else is in.** A few days a week, a strange substitute covers one of your classes. No warning: you'll find out when you walk in.
- **Meet the subs:**
  - **Mr. Crane, the Stickler:** late is a mark (twice); on time is rewarded.
  - **Miss Moth, the Dreamer:** time is only a suggestion. Open books, easier lesson, half pay.
  - **Signor Lazzaro, the Gambler:** Double or Nothing, or a Speed Round for bigger pay.
  - **Mx. Hollow, the Odd One:** never teaches the subject. Ever.
- **Two brand-new minigames you'll only get from a sub:**
  - **Candle Vigil:** keep every candle burning.
  - **Séance Sequence:** repeat the glowing sigils.
- **Errands:** some subs send you off with a hall pass to fetch something they left behind. Bring it back before the bell for an A.
- **Take a seat:** you can sit in the classroom chairs now. Every lesson game also has its own new icon.
