# Console controls (1.31.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

Grab a controller. Nightmare Highschool plays on a gamepad now.

**Fight**
RT strikes and LT blocks. RB is a heavy hit, LB a feint, and B dodges. Click the left stick to run and the right stick to crouch or slide. Crouch and strike for an uppercut. As a wolf, RT bites and RB leaps.

**Everything else**
X interacts and draws your weapon, Y carries a body, D-pad Up grips or drops your skateboard, and D-pad Down feeds. D-pad Left and Right flip through your hotbar. On a board, A ollies, X kickflips, LT pushes and the stick steers.

**Menus**
Press View for a quick menu with your phone, journal, factions, report card, wardrobe, emotes and settings, plus the controls. Every menu works with the D-pad: A picks, B backs out.
