## 🌕 Night sight, the full moon and a lot more atmosphere

👁️ **Night races can see in the dark.** Vampires, Werewolves, Ghouls and Wraiths press **H**. Your eyes glow in your race colour, and everyone can see them.
🌕 **Full-moon nights look the part:** a bigger moon, a cold crimson light, and a warning when it rises. Werewolves feel it most.
🕯️ **Getting back up matters.** A candle-gold wisp when you rise from being downed, and a clock sigil when Fate refuses to let you fall.
🔔 **Arriving at school:** a bell, a seal, and a card telling you where you are.
🌫️ **Teleports fade** through ink and smoke, and server hops get a proper "Leaving school..." card.
🚪 **Hide in a locker** and peek out through the vents. Everyone else just sees you vanish.
👻 **Watchers and NPCs fade in and out** instead of popping.
🎨 **Faction sigils** paint on with drips and scrub off in suds.
🧵 **Case files have red string.** Connect two clues and watch the thread draw itself. Solve a case and it gets **stamped**.
⚽ **Goals light up the pitch,** and **Bloodrush** has a new aura that matches your race.
🧹 **Fixes:** delinquents can be carried, gripped and finished. Students no longer do the prisoner pose in detention. Enemies walk around walls, and health comes back out of combat.
