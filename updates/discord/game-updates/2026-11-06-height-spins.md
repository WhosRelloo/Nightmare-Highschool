# Height spins (1.62.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-06**

- **Stand tall. Or don't.** Every student now has a height, and like your race and your clan, fate decides it. A new reel on the register rolls you somewhere between 5'2" and 6'5".
- **Your race sets the range.** Werewolves run tall, Ghouls run short and hunched, everyone else lands in between.
- **Both ends are rare.** Most students are Average. Short and Tall are Uncommon, Small and Very Tall are Rare, Tiny and Towering are Epic, and Pint-Sized and Giant are Legendary, 1 in 100 each.
- **Already enrolled?** Your student gets a free roll the next time you load them. Open them in the creator and watch the reel.
- **Spin again** with height spins: new students get two free, and more are coming to the Spin Shop.
- **It's only for looks.** Tall students aren't faster, don't hit harder and don't reach further.
- **The school isn't cloned any more.** Students in class and around the halls now come in all heights too.
