# Fists that flow, hits you can feel (2.8.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **Smoother strings.** Every punch used to hang on a little past its own animation. Now your combo flows straight into
  the next hit.
- **No more lost clicks.** Click during a swing and the next punch comes out the moment it finishes. One click, one
  punch; hold to keep swinging.
- **Contact you can feel.** Every hit lands with a short freeze-frame and a camera jolt, heavier on the finisher.
  Camera Shake in Settings still controls the jolt.
- **Parries take timing now.** Mashing block no longer keeps a parry up. Miss one and the next press just guards for a
  moment, so read the swing and press once.
