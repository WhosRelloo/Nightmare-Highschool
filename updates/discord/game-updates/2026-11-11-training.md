# Training: build your body, one area at a time (1.81.0, 1.85.2)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-11**

- **The gym has training stations.** Push-ups and pull-ups train your **arms**, sit-ups your **torso**, and squats your **legs**.
- **One button.** A ring closes on a brass gauge. Press when it hits the mark for Good, Great or Perfect. Watch out for cracked rings (let them pass) and leather-bound HOLD rings (keep holding).
- **Your body changes live.** Train one area and only that area grows, right there on the mat.
- **It gets harder as you get stronger:** faster rings, tighter timing, feints, heavy reps and burnout sets.
- **Come back daily.** Your first set of each area each day is worth double, a training streak adds a bonus and pays Yen at 3, 7, 14 and 30 days, every station keeps your personal best, and sore muscles tell you to switch areas.
- **Small, fair bonuses:** stronger arms hit a little harder, a stronger torso takes a little more, and stronger legs sprint a little faster.
- **1.85.2:** the real sit-up animation, and a pass of fixes (prompts up close, no attacking from the mat, gamepad buttons, layout).
