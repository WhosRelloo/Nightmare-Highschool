# New keys, a cinematic view and better cameras (1.11.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-02**

**New menu keys** (they're all on the right side of the keyboard now, away from combat and movement):
- **`** Locker
- **P** Options
- **M** Report Card
- **L** Factions
- **J** Journal
- **U** Study Group
- **Tab** Students
- **\\** Cinematic view

**Cinematic view (\\)**
- One key hides every bit of UI, including name tags, chat and the cursor.
- Black letterbox bars with gold trim slide in, the picture gets a filmic grade, and a title card fades in and out.
- Press \\ again to get everything back.

**Study groups live in the corner now**
- Your groupmates sit quietly in the bottom-left: race colour, level, health and posture, and how far away they are.
- A gold **+10% EXP** lights up when you're fighting together.
- Press **U** to invite or leave. Invites slide in right above it.

**Cameras fixed**
- **Hiding in a locker:** you actually look out through the vents now. Before, the screen went black.
- **The barber:** shows you in a lit three-quarter view instead of staring at his back.
- **Wardrobes:** light you up at night too.
- **Overlapping cameras:** two camera moments at once (say, a tier-up while you're at the barber) can't leave your camera stuck any more.
- **Locker inventory:** your character is framed head to toe.
