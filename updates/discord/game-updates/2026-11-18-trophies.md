# Trophies (2.33.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-18**

- **Nine trophies to earn,** each one modelled and finished in gilt, oak, leather and crimson enamel.
- **The Club Cup:** win a ranked club game.
- **The Honour Roll Plaque:** bring home a report card averaging an A.
- **The Ring Belt:** beat the Ring's champion.
- **The Register:** finish Chapter One. The page with one name struck through is yours to keep.
- **Five Path Medals,** one for reaching the top of each Standing Path.
- **The Trophy Case:** open your Permanent Record (M) to Honours & Trophies and watch them turn. The ones you haven't won yet wait as silhouettes, with how to win them.
- **Your trophy wall:** every trophy you win goes up on the shelves in your dorm room.
