# Gear up, burn hot (2.14.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **Hand gear at the armoury.** Fists have their own kit now:
  - **Hand Wraps** help you hold your guard.
  - **Weighted Gloves** crack guards open but slow your swings.
  - **Brass Knuckles** hit hardest, but they're contraband. If a hall monitor catches you fighting in them, they're
    gone.
- **Your race finishes the string.** The last punch of every combo carries who you are:
  - **Vampires** sip health back.
  - **Priests** smite the unholy.
  - **Werewolves** maul people across the room.
  - **Wraiths** chill.
  - **Ghouls** rot your guard.
  - **Humans** stand steady.

  On a Crimson or Blue moon, it glows with the moon's colour.
- **Heat.** Land hits, counter, parry and dodge to stoke the brazier on your health bar. Fill it, and your next skill
  is an EX: harder-hitting, and it can't be interrupted while it winds up.
