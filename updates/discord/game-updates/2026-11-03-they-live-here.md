# They live here (1.19.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-03**

The students and staff of Nightmare High feel a lot more alive:
- **They talk.** Walk past and you might catch a remark: gossip in the halls, a coach shouting drills, the hall monitor asking for your pass, a delinquent sizing you up. Some lines change after dark. Never more than a couple at once.
- **They move like you.** Same height, the same relaxed idle and walk, feet on the ground and smooth paths through the halls.
- **Their hair fits.** No more hair perched on top of their heads.
- **They dress for where they are.** Uniforms in school, street clothes out on the turf.
