# Skateboards (1.24.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

Get your board out. Press **B** and it slides in under your feet; press **B** again to step off.

**Riding**
- **W** to push, **A / D** to carve, **S** to brake.
- **Shift** to kick hard for a burst of speed, **Space** to ollie.
- Hit a wall too fast and you go flying. Everyone gets to watch.

**Nine boards**
- Everyone gets the **Moonlit** board free.
- The rest are in your Locker's new **Boards** tab, bought with Yen: Bone Hand, the Night Ravens, the Grave Rovers, the Iron Wardens, the Gargoyle, The Vigil, The Crimson Court, and The Headmaster.
- The Headmaster's board is black lacquer and gilt. Don't let him catch you on it.

**Heads-up:** B still grips a downed body when one's in reach. Your Grip key is your board key.
