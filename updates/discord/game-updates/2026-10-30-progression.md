## 📈 Nightmare Highschool: Levels, Stats & Bloodlines That Matter

**⭐ Level up**
• Earn EXP for going to class, passing lessons, finishing quests, winning duels, rising in your faction, slipping past hall monitors… and taking people down.
• School missions during school hours give **1.2× EXP**.
• 50 levels, with 3 stat points every level.

**💪 Three stats**
• **Might:** hit harder.
• **Grit:** more health, and your guard holds longer.
• **Wits:** learn faster and sneak better.
• Spend points in your **Student Record** (press **L**, or the book on the top bar).

**🩸 Your clan and traits are real now**
• Every clan perk and lineage trait does something: more damage at night, tougher skin, faster learning, healing when you're badly hurt…
• **Fated** students get back up once a day.
• Your Record spells out exactly what yours do.

**🕯️ Subtle EXP bar**
• A thin candle-gold line under your health with an ember at the tip.
• It fades back when you're not earning, and your level shows as a Roman numeral.

**👥** Students you know show their level on their nameplate and in the Students list.

*Class is in session. So is the grind.*
