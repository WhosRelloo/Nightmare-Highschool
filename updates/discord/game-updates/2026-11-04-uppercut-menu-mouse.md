# Controller uppercut and the menu mouse (1.33.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

- **Uppercut on a controller:** hold the right stick in and pull the right trigger (RT / R2). A quick click of the right stick still crouches, and the trigger on its own is still your strike.
- **The mouse is back on the menu screen.** It was hiding behind the menu, so you couldn't see what you were clicking.
