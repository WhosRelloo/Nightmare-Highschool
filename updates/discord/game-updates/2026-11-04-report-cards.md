# Report cards and new phone icons (1.40.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

- **Report cards are in.** Every Monday Mr. Hale sends last week's report card to your phone: your grade in every subject, your attendance, your conduct and a comment that may or may not be kind. Good marks pay an allowance, up to 80 Yen a week.
- **A Report Card app** to read it in: parchment, a wax seal with your average, and a bursar's stamp on your pay.
- **Every phone app has a new custom icon,** hand-made to match the school: a pocket watch, a sealed envelope, a hand mirror, a cathedral radio and more.
