# The halls come alive (1.13.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-03**

The school finally sounds and breathes like a real place.

**Listen closely**
- Every area has its own sound now: breeze and insects outside by day, crickets at night, the murmur of students in the halls, a quieter hum in class, the boiler's drone, and something faint and choral in the Archive after dark.
- It fades smoothly as you walk between areas, and follows your **Music & Ambience** volume.

**Look around**
- Candles in the Archive and on the altar flicker.
- Dust drifts through the Archive, steam rolls off the boiler pipe, and embers rise from the firebox.
- The pitch floodlights switch on at dusk and off at dawn.

**A cursor with a pulse**
- The gothic cursor is animated: the gem shimmers, glows over buttons and ripples when you click.
- New in **Options → Interface:** Cursor Size and Animated Cursor. Reduce Motion keeps it still.

**New outfits**
- **Seven new girls' outfits:** sailor uniform, chapel corset, bolero pinafore, cheer uniform, lolita dress, cable-knit with a plaid mini, and a gala gown with lace sleeves. Find them in the School Store and Wardrobe.
- **Transformations now respect your uniform.** Turn while you're in school uniform and you get a torn, clawed or stained version of it instead of a full costume change. Girls get their own Vampire (the Countess) and Wraith (the Banshee) looks.

**Cleaner HUD**
- The health bar is smaller and re-forged in dark engraved iron, so it takes up far less of your screen.
- The timetable card now sits neatly under the clock instead of stretching down the screen.

**Coming soon**
- Eight new pieces of furniture are built and textured: a grandfather clock with a swinging pendulum, wall sconces, hall benches, notice boards, a trophy cabinet, a school bell, a celestial globe and an alchemy table. They're on their way into the halls.
