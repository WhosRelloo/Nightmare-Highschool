# Stair blending pieces (1.50.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

- **A stone block that matches the stairs.** A new 4 × 4 × 4 block in the stair kit's own carved castle stone: the same grain, tint and cloudy weathering, with the same softly worn edges. Builders can now fill round, under and between the stairs without the stone changing colour or texture at the join.
- **The stair railing, on the level.** The stairs' gothic cast-iron railing (trefoil arches, gilt roundels, brass handrail) now comes as a straight 8-stud run for landings, balconies and wall tops. Lengths join end to end with no doubled bars, and the turned iron post with its gilt ball comes separately for the ends of a run.
