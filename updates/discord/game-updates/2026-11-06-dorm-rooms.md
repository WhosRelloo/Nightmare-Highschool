# Dorm rooms, and Mom & Dad (1.64.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-06**

- **Your own dorm room.** Every student gets one from day one. Walk through your door in the dorm wing and you're in your own private room, no matter who else is in the hallway.
- **Your room shows your grades.** Ace your report card and it's spotless: made bed, framed certificates, your beast trophies on the shelf. Slip and the clothes pile up. Fail and it's a wreck: pizza boxes, overdue notices on the wall, a dead plant and a flickering bulb. It changes on its own when a new card comes in.
- **Mom & Dad are watching.** They text after every report card. An A earns you a little extra allowance. A D gets a warning. An F, or two Ds in a row, and they cut your allowance off until your grades come back up to a C.
- **Have friends over.** Use "Invite to my room" in the phone's Classmates app, and they get a text to come visit.
- The room you see now is a stand-in; the real dorm furniture is on its way.
