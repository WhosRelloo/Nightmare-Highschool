**The gym is open.** A gothic stone hall south of the school, with a full court, bleachers, a scoreboard and a practice end. Two new fields sit beside it: a walled pitch and a floodlit football field.

**🦇 Join a club:** talk to the coaches.
**Night Ravens** (basketball, Coach Varga) · **Grave Rovers** (soccer, Coach Mallory) · **Iron Wardens** (football, Coach Dunmore).
Climb from Rookie to Captain and wear your club tag in the Students list.

**🏀 Basketball, Highschool Hoops style:**
• **E** hold and release to shoot. Hit the green on your meter.
• **Q R F C** dribble moves: chain them, cancel them, break ankles.
• **Z** euro step through contact. **G** post up.
• **Click** to pass where you aim. On defence, click to steal and jump to block.
• Phone and gamepad buttons included.
• 1v1 up to 5v5, a 24 s shot clock, threes, layups and dunks.

**🏆 Ranked club games** after school, with records, MVPs, EXP, yen and popularity.
