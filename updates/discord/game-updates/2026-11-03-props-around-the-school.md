# The new props are around the school (1.18.2)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-03**

The furniture from the last few updates is now placed around the school.

**The halls**
- Marble pillars at the entrance, candle-lit pillars along the hall and carved pilasters between the classrooms.
- A gothic mirror, paintings, the Crimson Court and Vigil banners, vases and plants.
- An avenue of twisted and octagonal pillars leads in from spawn.

**The rooms**
- The Staff Room: lunch on the table, a wall clock, the staff pigeonholes, a tea trolley and a gramophone you can play.
- The Archive and the Records Office: a typewriter, books, lamps, a telescope and filing cabinets whose drawers open.
- The Boiler Yard: a boiler whose furnace door swings open, steaming pipes, a valve you can turn, coal, a cart and a workbench.

**The quad**
- Between the classrooms and the gym: a cloister arcade, the fountain outside the gym doors, clipped yews, rose planters, a sundial, the founders' memorial, an old well and an outdoor chess table.

**Sports**
- Club banners and chandeliers in the gym, bleachers at the pitch, and hurdles and tackling dummies by the football practice field.
