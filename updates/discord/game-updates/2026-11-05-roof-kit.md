# Roofs (1.48.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

A whole roof kit is ready for the builders: 37 new pieces to roof the school and the town.

- **Pitched roofs:** slate gables at three pitches, with stone gables, crimson ridge tiles and iron cresting. There's also a hipped roof, a tall tower pyramid, a mansard with round windows, a gambrel and a lead lean-to.
- **Flat roofs:** a lead roof behind a stone parapet with gargoyle spouts, and a castle top with battlements and a crimson banner.
- **Towers:** a fish-scale turret cap, a tall crimson witch's hat, an octagonal spire topped with the school's golden key, a lead broach spire, a green copper ogee cap and an onion dome.
- **On the roof:** gabled and round dormers that glow warm at night, ridge cresting and tiles, a hip cap, a corner piece where two wings meet, gutters and a downpipe, two chimneys, a weathervane with an iron raven, a bell-cote with two bells, a cupola, a glass lantern with candles inside, and a skylight.
- **Old and worn:** a roof fallen in down to its charred rafters, a mossy one and a patched one.
- **Finials:** a golden crescent moon, the school key and a carved stone pinnacle.

The slates, lead and copper stay sharp however big the roof is.
