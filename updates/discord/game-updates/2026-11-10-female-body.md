🌙 **Nightmare Highschool: a new Female body** (dev 1.68.0)

The **Female** body choice is now a full body, not just a torso. It still looks like a classic R6 character.

🩰 **What's new**
• The torso keeps its hourglass, now with a smooth curved waist, softer shoulders and fuller hips.
• Arms and legs are slimmer toward the wrists and ankles, but still full R6 size at the shoulders and hips.
• Same head, same height, same animations. Everything fits inside the normal R6 shapes.

👗 **Your clothes still fit**
Every shirt, pair of pants and skirt, plus your skin colour, hair and face, works on the new body: blazers, jeans, the tartan skirt and the rest.

🎓 **How to get it**
If you already picked Female, you have it now. To switch, go to the creator's **Look** tab → **Body** → **Female**.
