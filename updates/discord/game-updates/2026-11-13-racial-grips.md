# Racial grips (2.6.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **Every race finishes its own way.** Press N on a downed student:
  - **Vampires** pull them up and sink their teeth into the neck, with a storm of bats.
  - **Ghouls** feed while the ground cracks and bone spikes rise around them.
  - **Werewolves** pounce, maul and howl under a pale moon.
  - **Wraiths** draw the soul right out of the body.
  - **Priests** drive the darkness out of the unholy.
  - **Humans** finish monsters with a hunter's stake, and the monster crumbles to ash.
- **Your victim fights back.** They shove, kick, claw at the floor and try to crawl away, and a possessed body thrashes
  against the priest until the final word.
- **They stay in your grip.** The body is held against you for the whole finisher.
- **New effects and sounds for each race.** Rarer sub-races get a showier version.
