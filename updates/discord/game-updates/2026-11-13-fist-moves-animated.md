# Fist moves, properly animated (2.12.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **The Shove has its own animation.** You load up with your arms back, then drive both palms into their chest and
  send them flying.
- **So does the Body Blow.** The fist winds way back, the hips snap round, and a hook digs into the ribs.
- **The Clinch is a real clinch now.** From your guard you grab them by the shoulders, yank them down and drive a knee
  into the gut. They fold over it, stagger back and get their guard up again.
- **Everything flows.** Each move picks up exactly where your punch left off and settles back into your fighting
  stance, so the strings feel like one motion.

The new moves switch on once the animations are uploaded. Until then the old ones still play.
