# Lock on, charge up, and slam them into the wall (2.11.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **Lock-on.** Middle mouse locks on to whoever's in front of you; you keep facing them between swings. On a
  controller or phone your swings now turn you toward the nearest opponent automatically.
- **The delay.** Pause before your 4th punch: if they've been turtling behind their guard, it smashes right through.
- **Charged heavy.** Hold your heavy to charge it up in three steps. A full charge breaks any guard, but you can be
  hit out of it.
- **Wall splats.** Knock someone into a wall and they're pinned there for a moment. Follow up.
