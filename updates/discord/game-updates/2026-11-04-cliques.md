# Cliques (1.21.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

Every student at Nightmare High runs with a crowd now, and every crowd remembers you.

**Eight cliques**
- **The Vigil** and **the Crimson Court**, the two factions you can join.
- **The Athletes**, who own the gym, and **the Delinquents** on the Back Lot.
- **The Pack**, wolf-blooded bullies around a fire in the woods.
- **The Ordinary**, humans who just want to graduate alive.
- **The Chapel Choir**, priest-blooded kids with candles and very sharp crosses.
- **The Ink Club**, artists and pranksters with paint on everything.

Each has its own leader, rival, colours and things to say. Listen in at Break.

**Places to hang out**
At Break, and after school, students head off to their clique's spot: the Commons fountain, the Chapel, the Ink Studio, the Crimson Parlor (invitation only) and the Den. Five new places to discover.

**New faces**
Captain Rhys Thorne of the Athletes, Lucien Grey of the Chapel Choir and Wren Ashby of the Ink Club are waiting at their hangouts.

**Know where you stand**
Every clique has an opinion of you, from Hostile to Respected. Knock one of their members down and they'll remember, though their rivals might not mind. Check it all in the new **Cliques** app on your phone.

**Fixes**
- Students leaving class no longer climb onto the desks behind them.
- The athletes can actually get into the gym now.

More to come: cliques that greet you, taunt you and pick fights with each other.
