# The students have names now (2.27.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-18**

- **The school's students are 24 named classmates now,** three in every clique, and each one looks the same every time you see them.
- **Stop and look at one, and they'll introduce themselves:** warmly, coolly, sharply or shyly, depending on who they are. Start with a shove, though, and they won't tell you their name.
- **They remember you, even after you leave.** Shove someone and the next time they see you it's "You again." Share a snack they like a couple of times and they'll thank you for it.
- **How they feel shows.** A student you've made a rival stays wary of you; one you've befriended stays friendly.
- **Your classmates are in the Students list (Tab):** everyone you've met, their clique, how they feel about you, and a line about who they are.
