# The Library (1.50.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

36 new pieces for an old haunted school library, in dark oak, brass, gilt and crimson leather.

- **Shelves:**
  - tall bookcases, a gothic arched one and a corner one;
  - a double-sided stack and a half-height case;
  - a 14-stud case with a rolling ladder on a brass rail;
  - ornate end caps.
- **The restricted section:** a case of black books locked in an iron cage, chained shut with a padlock and a DO NOT OPEN tag. There's also an old chained library, every book on its own chain.
- **To read at:** a long reading table with green banker's lamps, a round table, a study carrel, reading chairs, a lectern where a bronze raven holds a chained bestiary, and the librarian's desk with the borrowing ledger and a bell.
- **Library things:** a 30-drawer card catalogue with one drawer pulled out, a map chest with the map of the moor, and a rack of *The Nightmare Herald*.
- **Curiosities:** a globe of an invented world that slowly turns, an armillary sphere, a bust of the first librarian in spectacles, and a moon-phase clock with a swinging pendulum. And a SILENCE sign.
- **Books everywhere:** piles, an open book on a velvet cushion, a scroll rack, a book cart and a returns box.
- **A dusty corner:** a bookcase gone grey with dust and cobwebs, one that's fallen over and spilled its books, and corner cobwebs.

Every book has its own binding and a readable gilt title: 142 of them, from the Encyclopaedia volumes I to XII to *A Grammar of Shadows* and *The Library Cat*.
