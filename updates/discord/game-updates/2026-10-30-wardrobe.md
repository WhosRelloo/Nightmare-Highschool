## 🪞 The Wardrobe is open
Press **K** (or tap **Wardrobe** on the left) to make your student

**Look:** Scholar, Chapel (gold cross pin) or Nightborn (pale skin, a red glint in the eyes)
**Uniform:** Night Form, Chapel Grey or Archive Navy
**Looks:** 6 skin tones, 4 hairstyles and 6 hair colours
**Keep your Roblox outfit**, or switch to the school uniform. Your outfit is kept safe for when you switch back.

You see your own character as you change it, and your look saves to your profile.
*Just a look: your race is decided by fate, not the mirror...*

Also new: the server screen remembers the campus you **last joined**.
