# Statues (1.46.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

Ten new statues are ready for the builders, all carved in the classic blocky shape of a Roblox character.

- **The Founder:** cast in bronze in a gown and mortarboard, holding the school's key high.
- **The Mourning Angel:** veiled marble, weeping into her hands, wings folded, on a tomb.
- **The Gargoyle:** horned and bat-winged, grinning down from the top of a column.
- **The Vampire Lord:** black marble, sweeping open a crimson-lined cape.
- **The Werewolf:** howling on a mossy rock under a golden crescent moon.
- **The Lantern Priest:** a robed priest with a crescent staff and a lantern that really glows.
- **The Hollow Wraith:** a faceless hooded wraith floating over a spire.
- **The Knight Guardian:** leaning on his greatsword, made to stand in pairs at a gate.
- **The Duel:** two fencing students frozen mid-lunge.
- **The Raven Scholar:** a student reading on a bench, with ravens keeping her company.
