# The real dorm room (1.64.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-08**

- **Your dorm room is furnished.** Iron bed, a desk under a tall lancet window, a reading lamp, a bookshelf, a fern on its stand, a big crimson wardrobe and a woven rug, all under a hanging glass lamp.
- **It still shows your grades.** Ace your card and the bed's made, your certificates hang on the wall, and your beast trophies stand on their shelf. Fail and the bed's a tangle, the wardrobe hangs open with clothes spilling out, there are pizza boxes on the rug, notices pinned to the wall, and the fern is dead.
- **Trophies stay on their shelf.** They only show while the shelf does.
