# The Hellhound (1.56.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

- **Something prowls the boiler yard after dark.** A hellhound, bigger than any wolf, its hide charred black. Fire runs down its back and tail and burns at its paws. One horn juts from its brow, a row of bone spikes runs down its spine, and its eyes burn.
- **It howls when it sees you.** That's your warning. After that it fights the same way every time: two bites, a gout of fire, a bite, a leap back, and a pounce. Learn it.
- **Watch for the Kanji.** Before it breathes fire or pounces, the death mark flashes over its head, the same one you see before a critical. When it shows, its aim is set. Step out of the way.
- **Then punish it.** After the fire it stands catching its breath, and after a pounce it pants. Those are your openings. Mashing won't stun-lock it. It shrugs off hits for a moment after each stun, so dodge and strike.
- **It won't kill you.** It can put you down, and you can always run. Lead it far enough from its yard and it gives up.
- **Bring it down** for a big helping of Combat EXP and Yen. If you're lucky, it leaves an **Ember Tooth**, a trophy that stays in your Locker for good.

*The first of six beasts. More are coming to the night.*
