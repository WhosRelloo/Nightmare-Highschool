# The everyday-carry kit (1.80.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-11**

- **Twelve new things for your pockets.** Each one is a real 3D item you hold in your hand, and each does one useful thing.
- **Getting around:** a brass **hand lantern** that lights the dark (mind the oil), a folding **campus map** with a "you are here" mark, a **pocket compass** that points at your next class, your quest or a pin you drop on the map, and an **umbrella** that keeps the rain off and slows a fall.
- **School life:** a **timetable card**, your **dorm keys** (on your hip, and your room needs them), a **hall pass** that keeps the hall monitor off your back for a minute, and **library books** that help you study.
- **Comfort:** a **thermos** of tea or coffee, a tin of **Midnight Biscuits**, a **first-aid pouch** and a **hand warmer**.
- **Nights got colder.** Stay outside after 9 PM and you'll feel the chill. Head inside, drink some tea or light a hand warmer.
- **Some nights it rains.** Bring an umbrella.
- **Badly hurt?** Bandage up.
- **Keep your keys close.** If someone knocks you down, you might drop them.
- **Shop at:** MORROW & VANE, THE WICK & KETTLE, the Office and the Archive desk. Every student starts with the map, the timetable card and their keys.
- Find your kit in the Locker's new **Kit** tab and pin up to three items to your hotbar.
