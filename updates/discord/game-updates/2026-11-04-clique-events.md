# Clique events (1.33.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

The cliques don't just stand around any more.

**Pranks**
Every so often a clique sneaks into a rival's hangout and leaves a mess: their tag on the floor, a stink bomb, or trash everywhere. Catch them in the act and knock them down, or keep watch and help them finish. Clean up a mess for some goodwill. Want in? The concession stand sells Prank Kits, 3 a day. Just don't let a hall monitor, or the victims, see you.

**Rumble days**
Every few days two rival cliques settle it after school at the Commons. You'll get a text the day before. It's three waves a side until a coach breaks it up. Pick a side: the winners take the losers' hangout until the next night.

**Dares**
Leaders who like you text you a dare each morning. Prank our rivals, win a fight for us, ace a lesson, light a candle in the Chapel. Accept it in the new Texts app, or ask a leader in person: "Got a dare for me?" Dares run out at dawn.

**What's going on?**
The phone's Cliques app has a new Today tab: the next rumble, who holds what, fresh pranks, today's feuds and your dares.
