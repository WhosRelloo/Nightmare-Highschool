# The Crypt Bat (1.61.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-06**

- **Something's circling.** Red eyes, needle fangs, and wings the colour of old blood, torn at the edges. Crypt bats come out after dark in pairs and hover right at eye level.
- **They take turns.** While one rakes at you with its claws, the other circles, waiting for its go.
- **Watch for the warning.** When it flashes, a bat either dives: it folds its wings and swoops straight through where you're standing. Or it screeches: a shockwave rolls out of its mouth, and if you're in front of it you're stunned on the spot.
- **Get out of the way, then hit back.** After a dive or a screech it hangs there for a moment. That's your opening. They're fragile.
- **Bring them down** for Combat EXP and Yen, and maybe the **Crypt Bat's Wing-Claw**, a hooked black claw that smells of old stone.
- **Fixed:** dead beasts no longer sink through the floor, and a beast swooping through you can't fling you across the map any more.
