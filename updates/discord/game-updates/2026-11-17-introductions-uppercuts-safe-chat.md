# Introductions on the map, uppercuts that launch, and safer chat (2.21.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-17**

- **Introductions work on the school map.** Every visitor gets a student name and clan. Walk up to a stranger and press
  G to introduce yourself.
- **Uppercuts launch again on the school map.** Uppercuts now send your opponent up into the air combo, and the slams
  and knockbacks that check the floor work there too.
- **Chat follows Roblox's rules.** If your account has chat turned off, the chat window stays hidden. Staff
  announcements, warnings and reasons now pass through Roblox's text filter.
