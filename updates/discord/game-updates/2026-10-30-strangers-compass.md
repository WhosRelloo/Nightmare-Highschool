## 🕯️ Nightmare Highschool: Strangers in the Halls

**👤 You don't know them. Yet.**
• Every student is a **Stranger** until you learn their name.
• Walk up to someone and press **G** to introduce yourself. They'll see your name over your head from then on.
• Faction members already know each other.
• Names are truly private now: nobody can see yours unless you've introduced yourself.

**📜 Students list**
• Replaces Roblox's player list.
• Grouped by faction: The Vigil, The Crimson Court, Unaffiliated.
• Press **Tab** (or the top bar's Students button) to open it. It stays out of your way otherwise.
• Strangers show as Stranger, of course.

**🧭 Compass**
• A slim compass across the top shows classrooms, faction bases, the Ring and the people you're looking for.
• Your quest objective always shows in gold, even when it's behind you.

**🎯 New shift-lock cursor**
• Wrought iron, gold hour marks and a crimson heart.

**⚔️ Polish**
• E now always draws your weapon, even next to strangers.
• Duels can't restart until both fighters step off the pads.
• Duel messages don't reveal names anymore.

*Introduce yourself. Or don't. Some students prefer to stay strangers.*
