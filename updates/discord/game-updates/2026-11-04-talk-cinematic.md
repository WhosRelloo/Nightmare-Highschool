# Talk cinematics and a calmer menu (1.35.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

- **Conversations feel like scenes now.** Talk to anyone and the camera eases over your shoulder onto them, with a little letterbox and a soft blur behind. It glides back when you're done. Reduce Motion turns it into a simple cut.
- **The main menu's clock calmed down.** The crimson glow no longer washes out the stone ring, so you can actually see the carved stone behind your student.
