# Racial grips are live (2.7.2)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **Every race's finisher is live.** Press N on a downed body:
  - Vampire: the Kiss.
  - Ghoul: Eat Body.
  - Werewolf: Maul.
  - Wraith: Soul Siphon.
  - Priest: Exorcism.
  - Human: Hunter's Stake.
- **The victim fights back,** locked in the grip until it ends.
- **The full effects are on:** custom sounds, bat storms, grave cracks, claw streaks, frost, holy runes, ink smoke,
  and a heartbeat on the victim's screen.
- **The grips work in the Map as well.**
