# Controller update (1.32.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

Your controller now works everywhere, and PlayStation players get their own button names.

**PlayStation or Xbox**
Every hint, the quick menu and Settings now say Cross, Circle, Square and Triangle on a PlayStation controller, and A, B, X and Y on Xbox. The game works out which one you're holding. If it guesses wrong, pick it yourself in Settings > Controls > Button names. On PlayStation, press the touchpad to open the quick menu.

**Settings**
Move a slider with left and right on the D-pad, and hold to keep going. L1 and R1 switch tabs. The Controls tab lists every controller button.

**Lessons**
- **Pop Quiz:** pick answers with the D-pad.
- **Match the Past:** flip cards with the D-pad.
- **Lab Mix:** play it on the shoulder buttons, L2 L1 R1 R2 from left to right.
- **Word Scramble:** place letters with Cross. Circle undoes, Square clears and Triangle shuffles.
- **Report card:** Circle closes it.

**Fixes**
- A controller can no longer quit a lesson by accident during the 3-2-1 countdown.
- Pressing a combat button in a menu doesn't swing or feint any more.
