## 🔔 Nightmare Highschool: The Bell Rings

**There's finally a school bell.** A brass bell rings when a period starts or ends, and a card tells you where you should be: the period, the subject, the room and when it ends. If you're cutting class, your class marker pulses at you.

**Hall monitors let you know.** When one starts chasing you, it lights up orange with a big **!** over its head and blows its whistle. Get caught and the world drains grey under a red **CAUGHT** stamp. Lose it and the **!** turns into a **?** 👀

**Big moments get a proper banner:**
📜 new quests, new objectives, quests complete or failed (with the story's epilogue)
🔍 clues sparkle when you find them, then fly into your Journal
⭐ level-ups stamp your new level, and everyone nearby sees the embers rise
🪙 Spin Shop purchases confirm what you got

**Respawning** fades back in like waking up at roll call.

Everything respects your Camera Shake, Reduce Motion and Reduce Flashing settings.
