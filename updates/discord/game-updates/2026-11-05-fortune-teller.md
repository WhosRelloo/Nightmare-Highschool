# The Ink Club fortune teller (1.45.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

- **Meet Sable Vey, the Ink Club's seer.** Find Sable at a candlelit tarot table in the Ink Studio.
- **Five Yen, one card a day.** Sable draws from a hand-painted tarot deck and tells you something true about what's coming:
  - a rumble brewing
  - a dare you forgot
  - the next crimson or blue moon
  - where something lost is lying
  - what today's special does to you
  - who's winning this week's superlatives
  - how your grades are looking
- **Riddles first.** Sable speaks in riddles, but if you're on good terms with the Ink Club, the cards speak plainly.
- **Eight original cards:** The Tower, The Fool, The Moon, The Seeker, The Feast, The Crown, The Scholar, and a card back you'll learn to dread.
