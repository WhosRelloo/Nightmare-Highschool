# Students talk like their clique, and they notice your clothes (1.94.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-12**

- **Every clique has its own voice now.** The Athletes talk laps and hydration, the Vigil talks lamps and records, the Crimson Court talks money and manners, the Pack talks moon and scent, and so on. Students with no clique still have a few words of their own.
- **What they say depends on how they feel about you.** The same student sounds different when you're Hostile, Wary, Friendly or Respected. If they saw you fight, they'll be cooler for a while. If you keep running into each other, they'll warm up.
- **They react to the moment:** brushing past, standing too close, a shove, a fight, a full moon, night, your race, being hurt, and you in a beast form. 2289 new lines.
- **They notice what you wear.** Dress in a clique's look and they'll say so. Wear a rival's colours and they'll notice that too. Each clique likes some pieces and mocks others, so check what's on your head.
- **Hangouts have dress codes.** The Vigil's Night Study, and the leaders' in-person quests wait until you wear the clique's colours, are on good terms with them, or are a member. If you walk into their hangout out of their colours, a member will tell you. You can still walk in.
- **Not yet:** the Map. This is in the Combat Test place for now, until the Map has cliques too.
