# Body types in the Map place (1.69.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-10**

- **All the new body types are in the Map:** Lean, Athletic, Muscular, Stocky and Heavy for guys, and Petite, Toned, Strong, Curvy and Plus for girls.
- **They're earned, not picked.** In the full game they'll come from training. In the Map, staff set them from the Headmaster's Desk (**F3** > Student > Build).
- **Switching between male and female keeps your build:** an Athletic guy becomes a Toned girl.
