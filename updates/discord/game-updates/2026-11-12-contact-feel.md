# Bumping into students has weight (1.97.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-12**

- **Students are solid now.** You can't walk through them any more. You brush past their shoulder and slide around.
- **Bumps hit back.**
  - Walk into someone and you stumble a little.
  - Sprint into someone and you stop dead and rock back. The camera feels it too.
- **They react to how hard you hit them.** A light brush gets a twist and a "Watch it!". A full sprint sends them
  stumbling, and they'll tell you exactly what they think of it.
- Turn the camera shake down in Settings if you prefer it calmer.
