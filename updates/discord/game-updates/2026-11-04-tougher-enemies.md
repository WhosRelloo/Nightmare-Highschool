# Enemies with a skill level (1.23.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

The street fighters got smarter, and fairer.

**Four kinds of tough**
Every enemy now has a difficulty, shown as crimson pips under its name:
- ◆ **Easy**: delinquent grunts. Slow to react, rarely parry.
- ◆◆ **Normal**: the Brute. Parries now and then and guards up after a few hits.
- ◆◆◆ **Hard**: the Gang Leader. Quick, feints and punishes a parried swing.
- ◆◆◆◆ **Nightmare**: the Ring Champion. Reads your block and parries most first hits.

**Fair fights**
- Enemies decide once per swing you throw, not sixty times a second. Weak ones really are weak now.
- Nobody parries while stunned: land a hit and your combo keeps going.
- Heavy hits are easier to see coming, so expect the strong ones to answer them.

**Fixes**
- Outrunning one enemy no longer leaves the others circling you without attacking.
- Enemies leave downed, carried and dueling players alone, and ignore anyone still on the main menu.
- No more swings from across the room, constant hopping and sliding, or side-to-side jitter.
- A knocked-down enemy no longer snaps upright mid-ragdoll.

Admins can spawn a sparring partner of any difficulty from the panel. Go find your level.
