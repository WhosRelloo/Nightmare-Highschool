# Lost & Found (1.42.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

- **Students keep losing things.** Look for a soft glint near the cliques' hangouts: a varsity scarf, an ink-stained sketchbook, a studded wristband, a wolf-tooth pendant, a ribboned hymnal, a dented lunch tin… and a couple of things nobody will admit are theirs.
- **Pick it up** and you'll carry it in your hand. Your clock shows what you've got.
- **Return it to its owner:** find a student from that clique and hand it back. You'll get a thank-you and a little standing with the clique.
- **Or turn it in** at the Lost & Found box for some Yen. Things nobody can claim pay a bit more.
- Up to 5 a day, so keep your eyes open on the walk to class.
