# The Grave Ghoul (1.57.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-06**

- **Something sleeps under the graves.** The Grave Ghoul: grey hide, long claws, a grin full of teeth. It waits buried, and you won't see it until you're standing too close.
- **When the ground starts to churn, move.** The death mark flashes over the grave, then it claws its way up out of the earth. Anyone standing on top gets thrown.
- **It fights low and fast:** raking claw swipes, then a lunge with both hands. The death mark shows before the lunge. If it catches you, it pins you and slams you into the dirt.
- **Bring a friend.** A hit on the ghoul while it's holding you makes it let go. Dodge the lunge and it stumbles, wide open.
- **Leave it alone and it goes home.** It walks back to its grave and buries itself again, waiting for the next fool.
- **Bring it down** for Combat EXP and Yen, and maybe the **Grave-Dirt Locket**, a tarnished trophy with someone's portrait still inside.

*Beast two of six.*
