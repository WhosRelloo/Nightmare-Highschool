# Clique quests and turf wars (1.28.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

Every clique leader has work for you now, and every clique has something to give back.

**Leader quests**
Each of the eight cliques has two quests from its leader. Light the Vigil's lamps after dark, run the Court's invitation across school before it's too late, swipe a snack crate for Bruno, shake down the Commons for Dex, or tag the gym doors for the Ink Club without a monitor catching you. The second quest opens once they like you, and it usually means picking a side in a scuffle.

**Three new faces**
Captain Rhys Thorne runs the Athletes from the gym. Lucien Grey keeps the Chapel Choir's candles. Wren Ashby rules the Ink Studio.

**Earn their respect**
Get a clique to Respected and finish its second quest, and you get:
- two titles to wear
- their signature look, one click in the Wardrobe
- backup: get jumped near their turf and two of them jump in
- discounts: 25% off at the concession stand with the Ordinary, 25% off skateboards with the Delinquents

**Turf wars**
Win two scuffles at a rival's hangout in one day and it's yours until morning. Their crowd moves out, yours moves in, and the region card says who holds it. They only need one win to take it back.
