# Sable comes alive, and everyone sits properly (1.46.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

- **Sable moves now.** The Ink Club's seer breathes, reads the cards, looks up at you when you come close, and leans in like they already know your future.
- **No more sinking into chairs.** Students sit *on* their seats now: no more legs vanishing into cushions or heads through the staff room wall.
- **The Thinker** finally has a chair that fits.
- **No more standing inside the furniture.** Students hanging out with their clique used to stand inside tables, sofas, pews, easels and even the fountain. They've all been moved to clear floor.
- **Fewer wall-huggers.** Students walking around the school no longer scrape through doorframes and corners.
