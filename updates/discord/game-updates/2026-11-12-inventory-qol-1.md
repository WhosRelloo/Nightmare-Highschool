# Inventory upgrades (1.91.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-12**

- **Your hotbar remembers what you ran out of.** Finish your last onigiri and its slot stays, greyed with "Restock",
  so you know what to grab at the stand. Buy more and it's back in the same slot.
- **Cooldowns you can see.** After you eat, your food slots darken and count down until you're hungry again. Drinks
  show their wait too.
- **Snack buff on screen.** A chip above your hotbar shows your current snack buff, what it does for you (race twists
  included), and how long it has left. It flashes red when it's about to run out.
- **No more silent clicks.** Snacks tell you why they can't be used: still eating, or none left.
