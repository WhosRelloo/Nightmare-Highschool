# Windows and a glass crown (1.43.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

A big new kit is ready for the builders: 24 windows and a walk-in glass conservatory.

**Gothic windows**
- Tall lancets in soot-dark stone, a twin lancet with a pierced quatrefoil, a stepped triplet, a round oculus and a pointed iron casement with diamond panes.
- A great four-light tracery window and a twelve-petal rose window.

**Stained glass**
- Three original designs in crimson, gold and deep blue: the school's candle, crescent moon and key, and the Midnight Bell.
- They glow softly at night, lit from within.

**Every other shape**
- A classroom sash, a canted bay with battlements, a stone dormer, an arrow slit, a sunburst fanlight and an iron-barred basement window.
- A broken, boarded-up window, and closed crimson shutters with a crescent moon cut through.
- Plain clear windows too: a sash, a cross window, a wide picture window, a round-arched window, a half-circle window and a small square one.

Behind the glass, every lit window shows a warm candlelit room.

**The gothic crown conservatory**
- An octagonal glass hall under a faceted glass crown, topped by an iron spire and a gilt crescent moon.
- Eight stone spires stand round it, joined to the hall by flying buttresses.
- Inside are climbing crimson roses, ferns, iron urns and a ring of candles. You can walk in.
