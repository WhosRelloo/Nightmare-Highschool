# The daily special, the superlatives and the notice board (1.39.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

- **A new lunch special every school day.** Garlic Bread, Mystery Meat Loaf, Moon Cake, Holy Water Lemonade and Ectoplasm Jelly, one each a day for 30 Yen. Each one hits some races differently: Vampires really shouldn't eat Monday's.
- **The Midnight Bell's superlatives.** Every week the paper crowns the Class Clown, Most Feared, Teacher's Pet and more, from what you actually did that week across every server. Win one and you wear the title all next week.
- **A real notice board in the Main Hall.** Today's special, the superlatives, rumble news and today's dares, live on the board. Hit "Read" and your phone opens to the paper.
