# New clothes, split by gender (1.73.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-10**

- **62 new clothing pieces.** Every texture has clean edges: no black lines at the corners of your arms, legs or torso.
- **Schoolgirl kit:** 12 tops and 10 skirts. There are blazers, sweater vests, a cropped cardigan, a mourning pinafore, a choir capelet and a delinquent long blazer, with pleats, tartans, knee socks and tights.
- **Civilian kit, per gender:**
  - Girls get a velvet corset, a moto jacket, an off-shoulder moon sweater, a lace blouse, a cropped hoodie, flares, a velvet midi and a leather mini.
  - Boys get a wool overcoat, a satin bomber, a denim jacket with a hoodie, a henley with brass suspenders, chinos, high-tops and more.
- **Clothes follow your body now.** The Wardrobe and the School Store only show your gender's clothes, plus a few that suit everyone: the hoodie, the band tee, the PE shirt, the varsity jacket, the joggers and plain jeans.
  - Switch body and your outfit swaps to its other version, for example Black Blazer to Crest Blazer.
  - A school top you bought counts for both versions.
- **Girls' clique looks.** Every clique's signature look has a girls' version.
- **New girls enrol** in the Crest Blazer, the Navy Sweater Vest or the Cropped Cardigan, with charcoal pleats.
- **Staff outfits:** 8 outfits for admins only, among them a Night Warden greatcoat, a crimson haori with hakama, an exorcist's coat, a reaper's cloak, a council captain's coat and a duelist's cape. They have wool, velvet, leather, brass and embroidery you can see the shine and folds of.
