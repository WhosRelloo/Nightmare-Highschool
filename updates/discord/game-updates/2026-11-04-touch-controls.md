# Touch controls (1.29.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

You can play on your phone now.

**Fight with your thumbs**
Strike, block, dodge, heavy hits, feints, running and sliding all have buttons around the jump button. Hold crouch and strike for an uppercut. As a wolf, strike bites and heavy leaps.

**Only what you need**
The outer buttons change with what you're doing. Stand over someone who's down and you get grip, carry and your race's feed. Vampires drain, ghouls and werewolves feed, wraiths siphon, and humans and priests exorcise. Night races get transform and night sight. If there isn't room, MORE holds the rest.

**Skate on mobile**
Tap BOARD to drop your skateboard. Steer with the thumbstick, hold PUSH to pick up speed, and tap OLLIE or FLIP. The jump button ollies too.

**Out of the way**
The buttons hide during conversations, lessons, sports and when you're down, and the health bar slides over to make room for them. Cinematic mode has a return button now, so you can get back from it on a phone.
