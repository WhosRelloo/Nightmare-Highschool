# Lunch, down to the crumbs (1.95.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-12**

- **Watch your lunch disappear.** Every lunch tray now goes from full, to half eaten, to nearly gone, to an empty tray of dirty dishes as you eat it: noodles slurped, curry scraped, a fish picked down to its skeleton, a drumstick down to the bone.
- **Clean up after yourself.** When you finish, you keep holding the empty tray. Take it to the **tray return hatch** (a stone hatch with a roller belt that carries your tray away) or a **tray trolley** (it fills up rail by rail as students bring theirs back).
- **Or don't.** Pull out something else and your tray gets left on the table in front of you. Someone will clear it eventually.
- Every meal has its own leftovers: katsu curry, shoyu ramen, karaage, grilled mackerel, omurice, Witching Hour Stew, the Graveyard Shepherd's Pie, pumpkin gratin and Raven's Roast.
- Lunch trays arrive with the lunch period.
