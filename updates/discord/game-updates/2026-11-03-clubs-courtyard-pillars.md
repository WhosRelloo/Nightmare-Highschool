# Clubs, courtyard and pillars (1.17.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-03**

Forty-two more pieces are made for the builders to place around the school.

**Clubs and staff**
- Banners for the Night Ravens, Grave Rovers and Iron Wardens, and for The Vigil and The Crimson Court. They sway gently on their rods.
- A chess table with a full carved set, an artist's easel, a brass telescope, and a gramophone whose record spins when you play it.
- For the staff room: an iron filing cabinet with a drawer that opens, a typewriter, a tea trolley and the staff pigeonholes.

**Sports and the boiler room**
- A ball rack, hurdles, a tackling dummy, gym mats, a water keg and a gothic scoreboard.
- A riveted boiler whose furnace door swings open on the fire, steaming pipes, a valve wheel you can turn, coal, a coal cart and a workbench.

**The courtyard**
- A gothic fountain with grotesque spouts and a brass raven.
- A cloister arch, with or without its hanging lantern.
- A rose planter, a clipped yew, a sundial with a crow, an old well and a pierced stone parapet.
- The founders' memorial spire, lit by votive candles.

**Pillars**
- Eight castle pillars: clustered, octagonal, twisted marble, fan-vaulted, candle-niched, black marble with gilt, cast iron, and a wall pilaster.

You'll start finding them around the school as they're placed.
