# Foundations for the expansion (2.22.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-17**

- **New students get pointed to the Enrollment desk.** New Kid now starts with "Enrol at the desk and pick your uniform", with a marker to the desk. If you're already enrolled, nothing changes for you.
- **Your first quest waits until you walk into school,** so it no longer pops up while you're still making your student.
- **Night is the same night everywhere.** Lamps, wolves, beasts, the moon, hunger and the cold all keep their hours, now read from one school clock.
- **Conversations hand the camera back cleanly**, even if something else wants the camera mid-chat.
- **Clique standing updates straight away** on your screen.
- **The Midnight Bell no longer claims the hall monitors patrol at night.** They don't. Something else keeps the Records Office.
