# Behind the scenes: dress up on the new map (1.58.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-06**

- **Shift lock feels right on the new map:** Left Alt puts the camera over your shoulder with the game's own crosshair, and sprinting no longer flips it on by accident.
- **Make your student on the map:** press K anywhere to open the wardrobe. Change your face, hair, uniform, cape and accessories, and see how your look sits against the new school.
- It's a preview space: nothing you change there is saved, and your real character is untouched.
