# Admin capes (1.76.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-10**

- **Staff get real capes.** Five new 3D capes go with the admin outfits. They have real shape and real materials, not a painted-on picture: wool you can see the weave of, crushed velvet, satin that catches the light, and polished brass and silver.
- **Night Warden's Mantle:** a charcoal shoulder cape with a brass-edged collar, crimson piping and the Warden's key hanging from its clasp.
- **Reaper's Tattered Shroud:** a weathered cloak with the hood down, a cowl, and a torn hem greyed with ash. Look for the crimson-stitched mends.
- **Duelist's Shoulder Cape:** crimson velvet over one shoulder, edged in gold embroidery, with a gold cord across the chest.
- **Council Captain's Greatcape:** long crimson wool held by brass epaulettes, with the Council crest embroidered in gold on the back.
- **Midnight Lord's High-Collar Cape:** black velvet with a hidden pattern of bats and crescents, a crimson lining, a towering collar and a bat-wing hem.
- They all **swing as you walk, lag when you turn and flutter when you jump**, like the Spin Shop capes.
- Staff only: students won't find these in the Wardrobe or the Spin Shop.
