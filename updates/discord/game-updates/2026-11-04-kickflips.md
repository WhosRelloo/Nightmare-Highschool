# Kickflips (1.26.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

On your skateboard, press **E** for a kickflip. You pop up higher than an ollie, the board spins a full turn under your feet, and you land back on it. Everyone around you sees it.

While you're riding, interact prompts are off, so E is all yours. Step off with **B** and they come back.
