# The Bell Wheel: your snacks, one hold away (1.96.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-12**

- **Hold G (or D-pad Right) for the snack wheel.** Every snack you carry, up to 12, round a cast-iron and brass wheel
  with a stained-glass rose window. Aim at one and let go to eat or drink it. A quick tap of G still introduces you.
- **Best food.** Let go in the middle and it picks the cheapest food that covers the health you're missing, so you
  don't waste the good bento on a scratch.
- **It moves.** The wheel springs open, the sockets fan out, a clock hand swings to what you're aiming at, runes turn
  and glow, embers drift off the rim, and your snack drops into the middle as it folds shut.
- **At a glance:** each snack shows how many you have, its rarity, and its cooldown draining away.
