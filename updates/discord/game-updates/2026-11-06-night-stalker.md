# The Night Stalker (1.59.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-06**

- **Something tall walks the grounds after dark.** Nine feet of dark, wiry muscle, arms that hang past its knees, and a head with no eyes, only a grin. Shadows curl off it like smoke.
- **It only moves when you're not looking.** Keep your eyes on it and it stands frozen, mid-step. Look away, even for a second, and it's closer. Much closer.
- **It fights long.** A wide sweep, an overhead chop, and then the death mark flashes over its head: it lunges and spears one arm out further than you'd believe. Step out of the way and it's left wide open.
- **Hunt it with a friend.** One of you watches it while the other hits it.
- **Bring it down** for Combat EXP and Yen, and maybe the **Stalker's Finger-Bone**, a trophy far too long to be human that never warms in your hand.

*Beast three of six.*
