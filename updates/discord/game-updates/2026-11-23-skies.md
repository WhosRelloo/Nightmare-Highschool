# Skies and lighting (2.39.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-23**

- **19 painted skies.** The sky over the school is hand-painted for every part of the day.
- **Seven times of day:** Dawn, Morning, Day, Golden hour, Dusk, Night and the Witching hour, each with its own light and clouds.
- **The moon matters.** Full, new and crimson moons each change the night. A crimson moon washes the grounds red.
- **Seasons.** Event seasons (Winter, Spring, Summer and Nightmare) bring their own skies.
- **Clouds drift** with the wind, faster in a storm.
- **A brighter night.** Night is bluer and easier to see in, without losing the dark.
