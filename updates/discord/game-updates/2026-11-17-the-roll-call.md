# The roll call (2.24.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-17**

- **Chapter One now opens with a cutscene.** On your first morning the bell rings and you hurry to Room 102. Mr. Hale reads the register, and you answer to your own name. Then he reads one more name, and nobody answers.
- **New students get Orientation:** report to Mr. Hale, answer his question about the roll call, and find the empty desk in the back row. Then New Kid starts as before. Students who already started keep their progress.
- **Mr. Hale** now keeps his homeroom desk in Room 102. Ask him about the roll call. Don't bother asking about the room.
- **The Night Custodian** sweeps the main hallway by day. Nobody has seen his face. After hours, his post is empty.
- **After Hours has a new ending.** Read Mira's file and see for yourself.
- **Cutscenes you've seen** can be watched again from the Journal (J), under Cutscenes, and you can always skip a replay.
- **Watching with your study group?** Skip is a vote, and anyone who has already seen the cut votes to skip.
- **Disconnected mid-cutscene?** It plays again when you're back, with Skip offered.
