# After dark (2.30.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-18**

- **The PA is live.** A desk bell chimes and the announcement types itself over the clock: the morning bell, today's substitute, lunch, dismissal, and where a rumble is happening. It only ever announces what's really going on.
- **Night FM is remade.** It's a proper music app now: a home page, a library of your saved and recent tracks, a Now Playing screen with a scrubber, and likes that are saved to your slot.
- **A new look for it:** black lacquer, gilt rims, glowing purple enamel, twelve painted covers and big, clear icons.
- **The Midnight Broadcast has a voice.** Tune in to the Station tab and the host talks to you. Your first night gets a proper introduction, later nights a welcome back, and the sign-off comes just before 3 AM.
- **Listen at the right hour.** The hall monitors read the morning and after-school bulletins, there's a public service notice before curfew, and late at night the host starts talking about a student called Mira Holt, and an old photograph from 1887.
- **The station's feed is real too:** campus reports when something serious happens, caller lines when a rumour has gone round, and, some nights, a transmission nobody can explain.
- **The Gazette reads the halls.** Its Whispers quote the rumours going round right now, and a new On Campus section lists today's reports.
- **Curfew starts at 10 PM.** The PA calls it, and the hall monitor walks the halls until 6 AM. Get caught in the building and it goes on your record as trespassing, and any brass knuckles you're carrying are confiscated. Get back to the dorms and you're safe.
