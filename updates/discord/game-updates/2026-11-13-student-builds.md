# Students built by their clique (2.3.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **No two students the same size.** Every student's height and body now come from their clique. The Athletes are the
  tallest and broadest in school, the Pack runs long and rangy, and the Ink Club and the Ordinary kids are the slightest.
- **You can read them from across the hall.** Timid students hunch a little and keep their heads down. Brave ones stand
  tall and walk with purpose. The Crimson Court stands upright, chin up, and never hurries.
- **They stay themselves.** A student is the same height and build every time you see them.
- **The Map gets it too.** Students on the new map now have heights for the first time, and the same builds.
