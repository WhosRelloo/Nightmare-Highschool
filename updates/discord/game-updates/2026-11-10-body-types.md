💪 **Nightmare Highschool: Body types** (dev 1.69.0)

Your body can change now, but only by earning it. Ten new body types are coming with the training system.

🏋️ **Three ways to train**
• **Skinny:** Lean / Petite
• **Conditioned:** Athletic → Muscular / Toned → Strong
• **Heavy:** Stocky → Heavy / Curvy → Plus

🎨 **Still R6**
Every body keeps the classic R6 look: same head, same height, same animations. They're shaped with real detail: abs and V-lines, shoulder blades, big arms and calves, bellies and curves.

👕 **Your look still fits**
Clothes, skin colour, hair and faces all work on every body.

🔒 **Earned, not picked**
The character creator still lets you choose Male or Female, and your build carries over. The build itself only comes from training.
