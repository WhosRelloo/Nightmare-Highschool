# Real downslams (2.19.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **Downslams that go down.** The fist air heavy now hangs for a beat and drives straight into the ground in front of
  you, instead of gliding off across the room. In an air combo, the heavy and the final punch slam your opponent down
  where they are.
- **Enders finish their swing.** The shove and the body blow play out in full before your next move.
- **Staff can spawn enemies on the school map:** delinquents, brutes, gang leaders, crews and wolf packs, right in front
  of them, for testing fights.
