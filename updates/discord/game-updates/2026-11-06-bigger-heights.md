# Bigger heights (1.63.2)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-06**

- **The tall really are tall now.** Heights reach all the way to **7'0"**, and the short end goes down to 4'9".
- **Average stays average.** Most students are still close to a normal R6, but every rarer roll stands out more: Tall, Very Tall and Towering students tower over the crowd, and a Giant is a head and more above everyone.
- **Werewolves run tallest** on average, and Ghouls shortest.
- **Already rolled?** Your rarity stays the same, and your student grows or shrinks to match the new ranges.
