# The concession stand, upgraded (1.92.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-12**

- **Lunch is served.** From 11:25 to 12:00 on school days, Mrs. Okada sells the lunch meals: katsu curry, ramen,
  the Witching Hour Stew and more. The stand counts down to the next lunch.
- **Tabs:** Hot food, Snacks, Sweets, Drinks and Lunch, with today's special up front.
- **Buy as many as you want:** a - / + / Max picker on every item, with the total on the button.
- **Restock my pins:** one button tops up every snack on your hotbar to 5, and it shows the price first.
- **You can see what you can't buy:** items you can't afford are dimmed, and full stacks say so.
