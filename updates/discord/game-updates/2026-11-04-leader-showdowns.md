# Leader showdowns (1.34.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

Every clique has a leader, and now you can take them on.

**Friend or foe**
Get on a clique's good side and finish their leader's jobs, then ask them: "I want to test myself." Or get on their bad side, and the leader texts you: "Back Lot, after school. Come alone." Don't show up, and their crew will make your next day miserable.

**The fight**
It's one on one at their hangout, with their crew in a ring around you shouting. Get the leader to half health and they call two of them in. Put the leader down to win. Run away and it's a forfeit.

**The prize**
Beat a leader and their whole clique respects you. Beat all eight and you're **King (or Queen) of the School**: a title, a front-page story in the Midnight Bell, 10% off at every shop, and backup at every hangout.
