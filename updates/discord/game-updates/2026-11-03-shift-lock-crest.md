# A sharper shift lock (1.14.3)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-03**

**New shift-lock reticle**
- Shift lock (Left Alt) has a new reticle in the school's colours: a gold pierced diamond holding a crimson gem, with four small gold studs slowly circling it and a faint red glow.
- When you lock on, it snaps into place with a small pulse. While you're locked, it breathes. When you let go, it fades out.

**Smoother camera**
- Turning shift lock on or off now slides the camera over your shoulder instead of jumping there.
- If you flick it on and off quickly, the camera just turns around mid-slide.

**Your settings still apply**
- The reticle follows **Cursor Size** in Options > Interface.
- With **Animated Cursor** off or **Reduce Motion** on, the reticle stays still and simply fades.
- **Reduce Flashing** removes the glow flash.
