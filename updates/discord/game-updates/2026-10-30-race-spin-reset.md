## 🩸 Nightmare Highschool: New Blood Starts Fresh

**A race spin now starts you over at Tier I.** Each bloodline's power is earned through its own rites, so a new race means a new climb: your hunger resets and your old race's looks come off. Your traits and spins stay with you.

**Variant spins are unchanged.** You keep your race, your tier and your hunger.

The Bloodline tab now tells you this before you spin. Choose wisely. 🌙
