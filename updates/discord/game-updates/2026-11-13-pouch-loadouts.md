# Set up your hotbar your way: move slots, and three loadouts (2.7.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **Move your snacks.** In your Locker, drag a pouch slot onto another to swap its place. On a controller, pick a snack
  and use the arrows. Keys 1-4 always follow your pouch.
- **Day, Fight, Night.** Three saved pouches, one click apart. Keep coffee and onigiri for class, heavy food for a
  fight, the warm stuff for after dark. Each one remembers its snacks, and an empty one keeps its spot so Restock can
  fill it.
