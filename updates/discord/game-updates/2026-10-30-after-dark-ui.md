## 🕯️ Nightmare Highschool: "After Dark" UI Update
*The school looks different after the last bell...*

**New**
• **Gothic HUD:** a blood-red health bar with a clock-face orb, a hotbar for your tools (1-9 or tap), your Yen, and a posture meter
• **DOWNED alert:** a cracked skull-bell with your get-up countdown
• **New interact prompts:** hold **[E]** and watch the key fill with candle-gold
• **Server select:** live campus list with student counts, the current period and faction crests, plus Quick Join. Your own character is shown and updates live.

**Reworked**
• **Dialogue box:** smaller and lower so it stays out of your view, framed in the speaker's faction colours
• **Factions panel (P):** ornate cards, iron progress bars and engraved titles
• **Clock, notifications and downed prompts:** all redone in the new style
• **Lessons:** the chalkboard now hangs in an iron gothic frame

**Fixed**
• The backpack bar no longer comes back after a lesson

**Coming soon:** a quest journal and tracker reskin, plus the posture system

*All art is original and made for Nightmare Highschool.*
