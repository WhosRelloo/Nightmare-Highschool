# Know where you stand (1.16.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-03**

The school finally tells you where you are.

**Place names.** Walk into a room, a hall or a club's ground and its name burns in under the compass, with a line of flavour beneath it. *Chemistry: something on the back shelf is still bubbling.*

**Some places don't want you there.** The Staff Room, the Records Office and faction turf show in crimson, and their names flicker like a candle. The Archive and the Boiler Yard are fine by day. After dark they turn red and warn you off.

**Discover them all.** Each place you find for the first time gets the wax seal stamped beside it, a soft bell, EXP and a pip on your tally. There are 19 places to find on each save slot. Find them all and you earn the title **Explorer**.

**Also:**
- The compass names the place you're in when you're not facing anything.
- Place names still show in cinematic view, under the letterbox, for clean shots.
- The wax seal has new hand-pressed art.
