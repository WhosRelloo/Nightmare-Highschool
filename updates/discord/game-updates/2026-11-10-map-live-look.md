# Change your look anywhere in the Map (1.75.2)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-10**

- **Press K in the Map and change anything.** Gender, skin colour, eyes, brows, mouth: every change now shows on your character straight away. Before, only outfits and hair stuck, and everything else snapped back.
- **Body builds still come from training** or the staff desk.
- **The main game is unchanged:** your face and body are still made in the character creator, and outfits change at wardrobes.
