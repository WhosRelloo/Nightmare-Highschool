# Lamps and lights, and new grass (1.36.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

The school is getting its lights. Twelve new fixtures are ready for the builders, and every one really lights up.

**Lamps**
- Gothic gas lamp posts, one with twin lanterns and an iron raven, and wall lanterns. They light up at dusk and go out at dawn.
- Hanging candle lanterns, a tall church candlestand and a votive stand of 18 candles in crimson glass.
- Two chandeliers: a gothic iron crown of 16 candles, and a gilt one dripping with crystal.
- A crimson silk floor lamp and a burning wall torch that throws off embers.

**For the stage**
- A Victorian limelight that casts a real spotlight, and a run of footlights.

Candles, gas and fire flicker softly. With Reduce Flashing on, they barely move.

**New grass**
- Seven new grass textures for the grounds: a moonlit lawn with crimson leaves, the clipped quad lawn, overgrown churchyard grass, dead autumn grass, mossy turf, trodden paths and frost.
