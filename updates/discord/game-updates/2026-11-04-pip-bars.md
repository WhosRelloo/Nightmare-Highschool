# Wrought-iron pips (1.25.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

The difficulty pips over every enemy now sit in a little wrought-iron bar, cut from the same metal as your health bar.

The metal tells you the rank before you even count the pips:
- **Easy**: dull iron
- **Normal**: polished iron
- **Hard**: dark gold, with embers under the pips
- **Nightmare**: gold, with the pips burning bright

Same tags, same names, a lot more Nightmare High.
