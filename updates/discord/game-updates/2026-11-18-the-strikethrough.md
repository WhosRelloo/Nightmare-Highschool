# The Strikethrough (2.26.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-18**

- **After Hours now ends in a fight.** Bring Mira's file back to her desk in Room 102 at night, and something that was crossed out of the register climbs out of it.
- **The fight is a cutscene you play.** Dodge its lunge, shove it off you, win a tug of war over the file, then hold and release to finish it.
- **Every miss changes the scene, and the scene goes on.** Get thrown across the aisle, get grabbed, lose half the page. You always reach the end, but not always the same one.
- **What happens to the file stays with you.** Lose the tug of war and COLLECTED shows what's left of Mira's record: half a page.
- **It's graded S to D.** A good grade pays Yen the first time, and you can watch it again from the Journal (J).
- **Already partway through After Hours?** Your progress is kept, and the fight is waiting at the end. If you'd already reached COLLECTED, you stay there.
