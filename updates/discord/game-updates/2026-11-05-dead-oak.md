# The dead oak (1.52.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

- **Something old stands on the grounds.** A great dead oak, its roots gripping the earth, its limbs twisted into claws, one snapped off at the break, a black hollow in its trunk.
- **It isn't quite dead.** It creaks and sways, and now and then a groan shudders through it. Its branches slowly curl like grasping hands, and let go.
- **And it notices you.** Walk too close and a shiver runs through it. The trunk leans your way, and the limbs nearest you reach for you. Walk away, and they slowly let go.
