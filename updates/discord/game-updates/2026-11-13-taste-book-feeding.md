# The taste book, and feeding students (2.2.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **The taste book.** There are 53 foods and drinks at the stand and in the lunch line, and your Locker now keeps track
  of every one you've tried. Taste 10, 25, then all of them for the titles Snacker, Refectory Regular and Iron Stomach,
  each with +1 max health for good.
- **Share a snack.** Hold food near a student and offer it. Every clique has favourites (Athletes go for the yakisoba
  bun, the Choir for moon cake, the Pack for anything meaty). Hand over a favourite and their clique likes you a little
  more, once a day.
- **They talk back.** Students take it (or don't) in their own clique's voice. Someone who can't stand you won't take
  your food.
