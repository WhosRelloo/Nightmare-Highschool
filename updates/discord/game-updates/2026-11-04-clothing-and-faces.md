# Clean clothes, transformed outfits and scarier faces (1.37.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

- **No more black outlines on clothes.** Every outfit had a dark border round each arm, leg and torso side, most obvious on pale clothes. All 69 outfits are redrawn clean. Same designs, smooth seams.
- **Your outfit transforms with you.** Every top and bottom in the game now has its own Vampire, Wolf, Ghoul, Wraith and Priest version, so you keep your look when you change:
  - Vampire: deep velvet with a crimson silk collar
  - Wolf: fur bursting through the sleeves and legs
  - Ghoul: rotted and grave-stained
  - Wraith: fading into cold mist
  - Priest: a gold-trimmed stole
- **Transformations look menacing now.** Sunken, shadowed eyes with a glow, plus brand-new mouths:
  - Vampire: a wide snarl with long fangs
  - Wolf: a full snarl
  - Ghoul: a stitched ear-to-ear grin
  - Wraith: a gaping wail
- **Better mouths for everyone.** Every mouth is re-drawn with real material shading: glossy teeth, a wet tongue and deep shadow inside.
- **Race faces.** Your bloodline unlocks its own looks in the Wardrobe Face tab:
  - face marks (stitched cheeks, claw scars, frost veins, a holy brand and more; freckles, scars and plasters for everyone)
  - race mouths
  - **Bloodline eyes**: your race's eyes without transforming
