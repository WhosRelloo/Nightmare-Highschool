# Join a clique (1.35.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

You can join a crowd now.

**Getting in**
Earn a clique's respect, beat their leader in a showdown, then ask: "I want to join you." One crowd at a time: the Vigil and the Crimson Court still join as factions, and you can't be in a faction and a clique at once.

**Moving up**
- **Hanger-on:** members greet you by name, and back you up anywhere.
- **Member:** one of them walks with you outside class and fights at your side.
- **Lieutenant:** two of them, and better pay.

Your rivals won't wait until they hate you, either: walk onto their turf and they'll come for you.

**Jobs**
Your leader has a job for you every day: win fights for the crew, pay back the rivals, or guard your hangout from a prank.

**Getting out**
You can leave, but it's a betrayal. They'll turn on you, and their leader will want a rematch.
