# Inventory fixes (1.90.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-12**

- **Your pouch can't get stuck anymore.** Running out of a pinned snack used to block a pouch slot, so "To pouch" would
  say "Unpin one first" with nothing to unpin. Now the empty slot waits for your restock, gives way when you pin something
  new, and clears when you click it.
- **The Locker behaves.** It opens instantly, a quick double press doesn't reopen it, it closes when you die, and it fits
  on phone screens.
- **Gamepad:** B closes the concession stand, and your cursor stays where you were after using, pinning or dropping
  something.
- **The hall pass is honest.** It only covers being out of class. Showing it while you're chased for fighting won't
  waste a punch.
- **Your thermos coffee won't wipe a snack buff you paid for.**
- **Plus:** clearer messages when something can't be used, and tooltips that tell food, buffs and prank kits apart.
