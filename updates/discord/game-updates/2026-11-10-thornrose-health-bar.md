# A brand-new health bar: Thornrose (1.77.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-10**

- **Your health bar is a briar of black iron now.** Two thorned vines twist along it, closed rosebuds cap the ends, and the fill is crimson velvet. An iron rose sits in the middle and holds your health in its glass heart.
- **It reacts to every hit.** The velvet drains away smoothly, a hot spark flashes where it was cut, the thorns flare red and the rose shudders.
- **Healing** sends a gold shimmer along the bar.
- **When you're close to death** the rose's glass heart starts to beat, and it beats faster the closer you get.
- **Your form shows.** Transform and the bar fades to your form's colours as the rose turns a petal: dark wine for Vampires, amber for Wolves, bone green for Ghouls and pale spectral blue for Wraiths. Downed turns it to ash.
- **The posture bar matches.** It's the same iron briar now, with little thorns and rosebuds at its ends, and it still cracks, glows and shatters on a guard break.
- **Comfort settings:** Reduce Motion stops the shaking, turning, shimmering and heartbeat swell, and Reduce Flashing turns the flashes off.
- Health and posture work exactly as before. Only the look changed.
