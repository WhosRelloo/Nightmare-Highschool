# Your phone has arrived (1.12.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-02**

Every student now spawns with a **phone** in their hotbar. Press **Y** (or pick it from the hotbar) and you'll raise it to your face, and everyone can see you scrolling.

**What's on it**
- **Wallet:** send Yen to a classmate. Pick them, type an amount, confirm.
- **Night FM:** paste any Roblox audio ID and play your own soundtrack. Only you hear it, and it keeps playing with your phone away.
- **The Midnight Bell:** the school paper, written live: tonight's moon, who's top of the class, the discipline report, today's special at the snack stand, and the latest whispers in the halls.
- **Classmates:** everyone in the building. Send them Yen or invite them to your study group.
- **Camera:** jump straight into the cinematic view.
- **Style:** pick a wallpaper (Crimson Ink, Violet Night, Blood Moon, Vigil Candle...) or use your own image.
- **Shortcuts** to your timetable, study group, factions and options.

**Also new**
- **Click the clock** in the top-right for your timetable: every period, where you should be right now, and how today's classes went.

**Fixed**
- The top bar fades back properly again.
- A behind-the-scenes error with combat movement on slow-loading characters is gone.
