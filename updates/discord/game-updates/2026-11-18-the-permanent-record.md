# The Permanent Record (2.32.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-18**

- **Press M to open your Permanent Record:** the school's own leather-bound file on you, remade from scratch.
- **Your race, your cover.** Humans get oxblood leather and gilt. Priests get ivory vellum. Vampires get black leather with crimson velvet and garnets. Werewolves get clawed hide, Ghouls grave-green leather, and Wraiths ash-blue leather with a cold glow. Each cover has its own slow effect, from candlelight to drifting moonlight.
- **Everything in one place.** Nine tabs: Student, Academics, Attendance & Conduct, Athletics, Combat, Factions & Cliques, Paths, Honours and Notable Entries. Every page is laid out the same way, and anything you haven't earned yet tells you how.
- **30 Honours to earn,** from passing your first lesson to beating the Ring's champion. Two are secrets, sealed until you find them.
- **The school keeps notes.** Notable Entries is a log written in the school's own voice: "Flagged by the hall monitors as Street Fighter."
- **It moves.** The book lifts open, pages turn, gilt catches the light, and new honours stamp into place.
