## 🏫 The Headmaster's Desk + wolves that fight fair

🗝️ **Staff get the Headmaster's Desk (F3).** It's a full admin panel in the school's own style: moderation (warn, mute, freeze, detention, kick, ban), student tools (heal, revive, bring, give Yen, EXP or gear), the school clock and announcements, enemy spawns, and fly, noclip and ESP for staff. Every action is checked by the server and logged.

🐺 **Wolves fight a readable pattern now:** Bite, Bite, hop back, Leap. Watch for the **red glow**: it's your warning before every attack.
↔️ Back off or sidestep during the tell to dodge a bite. The leap locks on when the glow ends, so step aside and it sails past.
⏱️ After a leap the wolf pants for a moment. **That's your window.**
🐾 Only one wolf attacks you at a time. The rest of the pack circles and waits its turn.

👥 **NPCs got a makeover:** every student, coach and faction leader has real hair, and nobody floats, sinks into the floor or stands inside a wall any more.
📦 **Gear chests** stand upright against the walls and face the room.
