# The Bells (2.37.0 to 2.37.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-23**

- **The staff are watching.** A misdeed a member of staff sees builds your Heat, and Heat rings Bells.
- **Five Bells:** Murmur, Noticed, Named, Hunted and The Tolling. They hang on a forged rail under the clock, with a line telling you where they're searching.
- **Ring enough and they come for you.** The hall monitors start hunting. Run from them and it adds to your record.
- **Summons.** You may be called to Room 106. A slip on your conduct row points the way when you're out of class.
- **Seen transformed?** Staff who see you in your true form log it as Exposure.
- **Heat cools.** Keep your head down and the Bells fall quiet.
- **While you have Bells,** the quest card folds to its title so the rail has room.
- **The quest card, readable (2.37.1):** titles are bigger, in blood red on the parchment, and hints sit on their own line.
