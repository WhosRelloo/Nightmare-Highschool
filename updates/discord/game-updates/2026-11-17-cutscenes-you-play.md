# Cutscenes you play (2.25.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-17**

- **Cutscenes can now ask you to act.** Press on the beat, mash to break free, dodge left or right, hold and release on the mark, or answer before time runs out.
- **The roll call is the first.** When Mr. Hale reads your name, answer on the beat. Miss it and he notices.
- **Alone, the scene follows you the instant you press.** With your study group, everyone plays, and the scene follows the group.
- **Every interactive cutscene gives a grade from S to D.** Your best grade is kept in the Journal (J), beside Watch again.
- **The scene slows while a prompt is up**, and Reduce Motion gives you a little more time.
- **A prompt's key never throws a punch**, even if you're mid-fight when the cutscene starts.
