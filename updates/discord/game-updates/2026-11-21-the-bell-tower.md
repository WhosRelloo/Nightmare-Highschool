# The Bell Tower (2.36.0 to 2.36.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-21**

- **A new clock.** The clock in the top right is now the Bell Tower: a timetable dial with its hand on the time and the moon in its window.
- **An iron plate** under the dial shows the day, the time and the period.
- **Your conduct at a glance.** A wax seal is tinted by your discipline level, and a detention slip appears when you owe one.
- **The Lead Card.** Your tracked quest sits on a parchment card under the tower, with fang pips for counted objectives and a J key for the Journal.
- **Easier to read.** Text in the cluster is never smaller than 20 px.
- **Follow-ups (2.36.1):** the pips no longer crowd the J key, the card keeps pace with the tower as it grows, and the detection label is larger.
- **No rules changed.** It's a new look on the same clock, conduct and quests.
