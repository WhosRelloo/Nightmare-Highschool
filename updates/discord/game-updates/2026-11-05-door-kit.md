# Doors that open (1.44.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

A new door kit is ready for the builders: seven doors that really open, and look finished from both sides.

**The doors**
- A crimson iron classroom door with a wired-glass window and brass handles.
- Pointed gothic double doors, iron-studded, in a carved stone arch.
- A wrought-iron gate with spear-topped bars and gilt medallions.
- The headmaster's black-and-gilt door under an iron sunburst window.
- A secret bookcase that swings open on a hidden pivot.
- A riveted iron boiler-room door that slides into the wall.
- A cellar hatch with stone steps going down into the dark.

**How they work**
- Walk up and press Open. The door swings away from you, whichever side you're on.
- It closes by itself after a few seconds, but it won't shut while someone is standing in the doorway.
- Locked doors just rattle in their frames.
