# Every punch lands in full (2.18.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **Whole swings.** Every punch in your string now plays out start to finish at its real speed, instead of being cut
  off by the next one. Running punches and jump punches too.
- **Air combos fixed.** After an uppercut you stay lined up with your target and turn to face them. All four air hits
  connect, and the fourth slams them back down.
- On both the test place and the school map.
