# The chat and the Factions panel, by candlelight (2.41.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-25**

- **A quieter chat.** When nobody's talking, the chat in the top left fades to just the lines over a soft shadow.
- **It wakes when you need it.** Press /, move your mouse over it, or get a new message, and its iron frame, candle-lit header and bar fade back.
- **New lines drift in** like smoke.
- **Who's Who After Dark.** The Factions panel (L) has a flickering candle, each faction's card is washed in its colour, and your rank is five pips.
- **It opens with a little ceremony:** the sections rise in, the bars fill and the pips light one by one.
- **Fewer buttons.** The Factions and Journal buttons on the left edge are gone. Open them with L and J, from your Phone, or from the gamepad menu.
- **Reduce Motion** stills every bit of it, candles included.
- **Soccer** now plays its new animations in live servers.
