# Furnishing the school (1.15.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-03**

Twenty new pieces of furniture are made and ready for the builders to place around the school. They're in sharper detail than anything before them.

**Around the halls**
- Oxblood floor vases, crimson roses and white lilies in glass, and dark-leaved plants in stone urns.
- Black-and-gold paintings of the school on its hill under the full moon.
- Iron wall clocks, oil lamps, clusters of melting candles, and stacks of old books with an inkwell and quill.
- Iron umbrella stands and hall stands, one with a scarf and satchel left behind.
- A tall pointed mirror for the corridors, and a two-tier iron chandelier with twelve candles.

**Lunch**
- Pewter lunch trays: one full (mash and gravy, mystery meat, peas, a roll, red jelly, an apple and milk), one picked clean.

**The washrooms**
- Black marble stalls with crimson doors that open and close.
- Marble sinks with brass taps that turn and run water.
- Iron mirrors and brass towel rails to match.

**For the Iron Wardens**
- A brass-and-iron steam throwing machine for the football drills, with a crate of footballs.
- Crimson-and-iron bleachers.

You'll start seeing them around the school once they're placed.
