# Quiet Corners (2.38.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-23**

- **A quieter screen.** The HUD eases back when you're calm and steps up in a fight, a conversation or a cutscene. Pick how it behaves in Settings (HUD mode).
- **Paper slips.** Notifications are slips of paper in the top left, newest on top, a few at a time, held until a fight ends.
- **One prompt at a time.** Only the nearest prompt shows its key cap and hold fill; the others are a small pip.
- **A new compass** strip with clearer letters.
- **Dialogue** opens on a calling card with the speaker's clique crest.
- **The Journal is a book.** Its tabs are Journal, Evidence, Record and Settings.
- **The Evidence Wall.** Case files are now a wall: pin your clues and link them to answer the case's questions.
- **The skip button** in cutscenes is your race's ring.
