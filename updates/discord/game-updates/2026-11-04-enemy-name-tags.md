# Know your enemy (1.25.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

Enemies wear new name tags, built so you can size up a fight at a glance.

- Their name, in the school's lettering.
- Their **difficulty pips**, big and front and centre, on a dark plaque. The tougher they are, the brighter the crimson outline.
- The tier underneath: **EASY**, **NORMAL**, **HARD** or **NIGHTMARE**.

A one-pip delinquent is a warm-up. Four pips with a blazing outline means the Ring Champion, and you should probably have a plan.

The tags read by day and by night, and vanish in cinematic view like every other name.
