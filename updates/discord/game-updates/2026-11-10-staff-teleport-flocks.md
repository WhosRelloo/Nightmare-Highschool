# Staff teleport flocks (1.75.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-10**

- **Staff now travel as crows.** When an admin teleports, their body breaks apart into a flock of crows that burst into the sky and scatter, leaving loose black feathers drifting down. At the other end, crows spiral in from all around and fold back into them as they reappear. Look closely and you'll catch a faint crimson glint in their eyes.
- **The headmaster travels as bats.** The owner gets a teleport of their own: a crimson sigil burns into the ground beneath them and they're torn away in a rising vortex of bats. Where they land, the sigil flares again, bats dive out of the night sky into them, and a few peel away as they stand up.
- **Brought by staff?** If staff bring you to them, you'll arrive inside a small swirl of their crows or bats.
- **Hand-made birds.** Every crow and bat is a fully modelled, rigged creature with flapping wings, not a flat sprite. The crows have fingered wingtips and fanned tails; the bats have tall ears, fangs and leathery wings.
- **Comfort settings:** with Reduce Motion on, a handful of birds simply rise or glide in, with no vortex or swirl. Low effects quality uses half the birds.
- Only staff can trigger these, and everyone else's teleports keep the usual veil.
