# Locker upgrades (1.93.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-12**

- **Sort and filter your Locker.** Sort by kind, rarity, how many you have, or A to Z. Tap Heals, Buffs, Lunch,
  Specials or Trophies to see only those.
- **Favourites.** Star anything (up to 12) and it comes first in your Locker and at the concession stand.
- **Drop how many?** Dropping a stack asks how many: 1, 5 or all of it.
- **Undo.** Dropped something by accident? You have 5 seconds to take it back.
