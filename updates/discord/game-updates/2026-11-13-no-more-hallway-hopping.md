# No more hallway hopping (2.6.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **Students walk the halls again.** In the crowd before school and between classes, students kept hopping, every few
  seconds, and some landed on lockers or on each other's heads. Now they just walk. When someone's in the way, they
  step around them.
- **No climbing on the furniture.** Students heading to the back rows of a classroom used to jump up onto the desks to
  reach their seat. Now they walk down the aisle and sit.
- **Steps still work.** On the new map, students still hop up onto the front steps and walk in through the doors.
