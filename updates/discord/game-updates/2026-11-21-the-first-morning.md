# The first morning, without the cuts (2.35.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-21**

- **No more cutting away.** In the tutorial, sneaking out and the bus no longer cut to a separate scene. The camera glides out of your view, plays the moment, and glides back in.
- **You're the actor.** It's your own character on screen. You crouch past Biscuit the dog, vault the fence and walk to the black bus.
- **Mind Biscuit.** Stand up too close to him and the bedroom light flicks on.
- **Boarding the bus** happens under the title card, still in one shot.
- **Wickfield is yours alone.** Each player gets their own copy of the first morning.
- **Safe to fail.** If you die or rejoin, you're back at your last checkpoint.
