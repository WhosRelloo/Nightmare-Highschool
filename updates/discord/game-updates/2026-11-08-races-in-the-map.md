# Races in the Map place (1.65.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-08**

- **Every student in the Map has a bloodline now.** Join and you roll a race: Human, Priest, Vampire, Werewolf, Ghoul or Wraith, with the same odds as the full game.
- **You'll look the part.** Vampire ears, Werewolf ears, tails and fur, a Ghoul's grave wear, a Wraith's cold wisps. Your skin keeps to your race's tones, and the race faces show up in the wardrobe.
- **Open the wardrobe (K)** for the new Bloodline tab: your race, your tier, your passive, your traits, and spins to roll again.
- **Night races see in the dark.** Vampires, Werewolves, Ghouls and Wraiths press H.
- **Staff can set anyone's race and tier** from the Headmaster's Desk (F3).
