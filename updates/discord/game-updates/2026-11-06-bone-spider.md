# The Bone Spider (1.60.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-06**

- **Look up.** Something hangs over the grounds after dark, head down on a single thread of silk: a skull for a body, a ribcage behind it, and eight long legs of bone. A red light burns inside the skull.
- **Don't walk under it.** Get too close and the warning flashes on the ground beneath it. Then it drops on you, lands on its feet, and comes at you. Leave it alone and it climbs back up its thread to wait.
- **It's quick, not tough.** Two fang strikes, then the warning again: it rears back and spits a glob of silk. Get hit and you're stuck slow for a few seconds. Move after the warning and it misses.
- **Fight back.** It's fragile. Dodge the spit, punish the opening, and it goes down fast.
- **Bring it down** for Combat EXP and Yen, and maybe the **Bone Spider's Fang**, a hooked fang with old silk still wound around its root.
- **Fixed:** a beast killed mid-attack could still finish its swing, and dead beasts no longer get back up before they fade.
