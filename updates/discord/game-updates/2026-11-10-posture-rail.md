# A new posture bar (1.74.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-10**

- **Posture has a new look.** The thin meter at the side of your screen is gone. In its place, a slim forged rail hangs right under your health bar, made of the same iron and brass. Your health orb sits in a brass cradle in its middle, so the two read as one piece.
- **It fills out from the orb.** Every hit you block pours molten brass outward along the rail. It glows hotter, turning from gold to ember, the closer you are to breaking.
- **Cracks spread as your guard weakens.** They start at 40%, spread further at 65%, and run the whole length at 85%. They heal again as your posture recovers.
- **A warning before the break.** From 80%, the gems at each end of the rail pulse like embers.
- **Guard break:** the brass shatters off the rail, and it reads **GUARD | BROKEN** until you can move again.
- **Comfort settings:** with Reduce Motion on, there are no flying shards and the gems glow steadily instead of pulsing. Reduce Flashing turns the flashes off.
- **On phones and tablets** the rail shrinks with the health bar and keeps clear of the hotbar.
- Posture itself works exactly as before: only the look changed.
