# Teachers take the register (2.28.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-18**

- **Meet the staff.** Ms. Sallow teaches Maths in Room 102, Mr. Hale Chemistry in Room 103, Mr. Pryce English in Room 104 and Dr. Whitlock History in Room 105. Each one has something to say if you stop by their desk.
- **Teachers keep the day.** They're at their desks during class and in the Staff Room between lessons. Mr. Hale still takes the morning register in Room 102, and that's where you'll find him out of class.
- **Headmaster Ashgrave keeps office hours** by the Records Office door during class time. Ask him about Mira Holt, if you dare.
- **Every class has its own classmates.** Six named students sit each lesson, and the groups rotate through the day. A full homeroom stands at the back.
- **Some Delinquents skip class.** You'll find them hanging out instead.
- **Teachers see things.** Start a fight in a classroom while the teacher's watching, and it goes on your record under their name.
- **The empty desk in the back row of Room 102 stays empty.**
