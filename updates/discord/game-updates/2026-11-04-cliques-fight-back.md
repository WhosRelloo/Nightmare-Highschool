# The cliques fight back (1.27.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

The cliques have opinions about you now, and they're not shy about them.

**They talk to you**
Every clique has lines for how it feels about you, from the Court's "Darling. Always a pleasure." to the Pack's "Fresh meat walked into the Den." Students who respect you, or can't stand you, turn to look as you pass.

**Turf**
Hang around the hangout of a clique that hates you and they'll notice. Give it a few seconds and two or three of them come for you. Walk away far enough and they'll let you go with a parting shot.

**Scuffles**
Rival cliques go at it at break, after school and in the evening: the Pack storming the Chapel, the Court and the Vigil in the Archive, the Delinquents and the Ordinary at the Commons, the Athletes and the Ink Club at the gym. Watch, or pick a side. Every rival you put down wins you friends on the other side, and more if your side wins.

**The hall monitor**
On school grounds a monitor comes running to break it up, and throwing punches in front of one still gets you booked. Off-campus turf like the Den and the Parlor is lawless.

**Tough crowds**
The Ordinary, the Ink Club and the Choir are easy to push around. The Vigil, the Athletes and the Delinquents fight fair. The Crimson Court and the Pack will hurt you.
