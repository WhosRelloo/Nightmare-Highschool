# The wolf form in the Map place (1.65.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-08**

- **Werewolves can turn in the Map now.** A Werewolf of Tier II or higher presses **T** and becomes the full wolf, with a howl.
- **As the wolf:** run on all fours, click to bite, R to leap and bite. Press T again to change back.
- **Testing it:** open the Headmaster's Desk (F3), pick Werewolf and Tier II on the Self tab, then press T.
