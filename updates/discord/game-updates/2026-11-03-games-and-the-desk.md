# Games on your phone, and a busier headmaster (1.18.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-03**

**Your phone has three new games**, each one ours, in the school's own style:
- **Wisp Flight:** tap to guide a candle wisp between wrought-iron spires under the moon. Touch the iron and the flame gutters.
- **Hex Slash:** swipe through cursed candles, blood drops, wax seals and bells as they're tossed up. Leave the holy cross alone.
- **Crypt Crush:** swap runes on a crypt slab to match them. Line up four for a sigil and five for a seal.

Your best score in each game is saved, and every game shows the **top 5 in the building**. Strangers stay strangers on the board.

**For staff**, the Headmaster's Desk now covers the whole school:
- discipline and attendance
- lessons and periods
- levels and titles
- places found
- clubs, matches and quests
- the full moon

It also has a command search, favourites and a read-only look at a student's record.
