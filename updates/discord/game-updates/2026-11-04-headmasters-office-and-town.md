# The Headmaster's office and the town (1.20.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-04**

Forty-two more pieces are made for the builders, and Mrs. Okada's stand is getting a makeover.

**The Headmaster's office**
- A black lacquer desk with a drawer you can open, a crimson throne of a chair and a fireplace that throws embers.
- The founder's portrait and marble bust, the school crest, a decanter tray and an hourglass.
- A safe and a cabinet of confiscated things, both with doors that open. Yes, that is a cursed doll.

**The town**
- An apothecary and a curio shop with lit windows and doors that open, a market stall, a swinging shop sign.
- A crimson postbox, a water pump, a news kiosk and a gothic telephone booth.
- Bollards, iron railings, a signpost with a crow, and market crates.
- Building pieces for the town's houses: doors, lit windows, bay windows, balconies, awnings, chimneys that smoke, dormers, drainpipes and front steps.

**Tables**
- Round, oval, a sixteen-stud feast table, café, hexagonal, half-moon, coffee and side tables.

**The concession stand**
- Rebuilt in the school's style: cast iron and oxblood under a slate roof, a striped awning, lanterns, and a marble counter of sweet jars and potion sodas. Same snacks, same Mrs. Okada.

You'll start seeing them as they're placed.
