## ⚙️ Settings menu is here
Press **M** (or tap **Settings** on the left)

**Controls:** rebind Block, Dodge, Sprint, Slide, Heavy Attack, Feint, Carry and Grip
**Audio:** Master, Combat Effects, and Music & Ambience volume
**Camera:** Field of View and Camera Shake strength (0% turns it off)
**Interface:** UI Scale, show or hide the HUD and clock
**Graphics:** Shadows, Screen Vignette

Changes apply instantly and save to your profile, so they follow you to any server.
