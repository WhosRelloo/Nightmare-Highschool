# The big bug sweep (1.53.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

We went through the whole game, every script, and fixed 45 bugs. The ones you'd notice:

- **Students actually leave class now.** They used to get stuck in their chairs after the first lesson, and the whole school stood still.
- **Combat is cleaner.** Attacks right after letting go of sprint no longer overlap. Getting stunned mid-sprint stops you properly. If someone grabs you and leaves, you're not frozen in place any more.
- **Sports:** you can jump again after being tackled or stunned. Vampires get their night accuracy in every sport. Football no longer freezes if the ball carrier goes down.
- **Cliques:** leave a clique and you can win your way back in. Showdowns are a real one-on-one now: no backup or crew joining in.
- **Wolves** respawn at the proper pace at night, and they stop biting you once you're down.
- **Detention:** getting knocked out or carried away no longer counts as skipping it.
- **New jump and landing animations** for everyone, and the jump now plays all the way through.
- Lots of smaller fixes to the phone, the quest journal, stealth, fortunes, Lost & Found and the menus, plus a few exploits closed.
