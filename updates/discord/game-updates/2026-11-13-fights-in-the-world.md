# Fights in the world (2.15.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **Crowds.** Start a fight and students form a ring around you. They chant, bet their lunch money, beg you to stop,
  and hold their phones up to record. A big enough crowd brings the hall monitors running.
- **Tag in your study group.** Mid-fight, press G to call your group. A groupmate nearby can answer and blink in for
  one hard-hitting assist strike.
- **Stagger the beasts.** Fists break a night beast's footing: keep hitting and it staggers, wide open and taking
  extra damage.
- **Enemies fight like you do now.** Most enemy students fight with their fists, and some carry brass knuckles.
