# Word gets around (2.29.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-18**

- **Things that happen at school don't stay quiet.** Fights, catches, pranks, match results, beast sightings: the students who saw them start talking.
- **Rumours spread through the school.** Stand near two classmates chatting and you might hear one: "Did you hear? Jax pushed Agnes into a locker in the main hall."
- **Every retelling stretches the story a little.** A shove becomes a slam, the hallway becomes the Commons, a different student gets the blame.
- **But never about you.** A rumour about a real player always stays true. Nobody can talk you into something you didn't do.
- **Six small scenes now play out around you:** an argument between rival cliques, a student shoved into a locker, a defaced notice board, footsteps in the east wing at dusk, a figure at the end of the main hall, and a dare at the Records Office door. Step in, help out, or just watch, and see how it ends.
- **Sable hears the whispers too.** Her fortunes may tell you what the halls are saying.
- **Learn a name the hard way.** If you fell out with a classmate before they ever introduced themselves, you might still hear their name in a rumour.
