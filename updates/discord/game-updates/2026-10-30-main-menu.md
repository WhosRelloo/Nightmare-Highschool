## 🕯️ Nightmare Highschool: Main Menu & Save Slots

The academy now greets you properly.

**🌙 A cinematic main menu**
• A night courtyard in front of the school: wet cobblestone, old brick, a clock tower that shows the real school time, gargoyles on the iron gate, and dead oaks hung with moss (someone threw their sneakers up there).
• Your student floats inside a crimson clock seal while energy tears out of the stone ring behind them.
• Flickering lamps, chimney smoke, rolling fog, bats around the tower, the odd lightning flash.
• A skippable intro fly-in, then smooth cinematic camera moves between every screen.

**🎓 Three save slots**
• Each slot is its own student, with their own race roll, record, factions, quests and Yen.
• Your current save is Slot I, so nothing is lost.
• Each card shows race, title, faction rank, Yen and when you last played. Empty slots wait as a crimson ghost.

**🪞 Make your student, on camera**
• Pick a slot and your student steps into the seal, with the Wardrobe beside them.
• The camera cuts in close for the face, high for the hair and wide for the outfit. Drag to turn them.
• Hit **ENTER SCHOOL** and the camera pushes to the doors as the bell rings.

**✨ New VFX**
Smoke, plasma tendrils, crystal shards and embers, rendered in Blender and brought into the game.

**Menu:** CONTINUE · PLAY · SETTINGS · CREDITS (keyboard, mouse or controller)

*Class is never truly dismissed.*
