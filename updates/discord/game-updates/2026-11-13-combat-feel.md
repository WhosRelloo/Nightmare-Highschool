# Counter-hits, and fights you can read (2.9.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-13**

- **Counter-hits.** Catch someone in the middle of their wind-up and it's a counter: a white flash, a sharper crack,
  and they're stunned half as long again.
- **Hits land where they land.** Punch someone from the side and they're knocked the other way; from behind, they
  double over. Trade blows head-on and the exchange rocks back and forth.
- **The uppercut warns you.** It now flashes as it winds up, so you can see the launcher coming.
- **Combo counter.** Your hit count appears at the edge of the screen while a string lands, and you can turn on damage
  numbers too. Both are in Settings > Interface > Combat.
