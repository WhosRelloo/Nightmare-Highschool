# Sofas, Rugs and Tapestries (1.49.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

30 new pieces to furnish the school and the town: sofas, rugs, carpets and woven tapestries.

- **Sofas and seats:** a deep-tufted crimson chesterfield, a black velvet camelback on gilt claw feet, a damask fainting couch, a gothic settle with three tufted arches, a shell-backed loveseat, an L-shaped common room sofa, a couch whose back is a coffin, a sofa with bat wings for a back, the Headmaster's throne settee with ravens on the arms, a tufted window bench and a round ottoman with a gold fringe.
- **Rugs and mats:** a moth medallion rug with cobwebs in the corners, a hall runner full of watching eyes, a thorn-rose round rug, a star-chart octagon with the phases of the moon, a raven hearth rug, a worn bat damask, two coir doormats and a bath mat.
- **Carpet tiles:** four patterns (damask, cobweb, harlequin and moths) that join seamlessly, for carpeting whole rooms.
- **Tapestries:** six woven scenes on gilt rods:
  - the Headmaster with the school's blazing key;
  - a priest holding back a demon with his lantern;
  - an angel with a burning sword against a demon in a storm;
  - a crowned sovereign as demons fall into the fire;
  - the school crest held by two ravens;
  - a wolf hunt under the moon.

Every design is original, and the textiles are woven right into the maps, down to the fringe and the gold thread.
