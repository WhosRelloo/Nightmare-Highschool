# The staff console, Soccer 2.0, the Infirmary and a new pace (2.40.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-24**

- **Soccer 2.0.** Every move has new animation: kicks meet the ball, a charged shot winds up, and dives and dribbles carry you as far as they look.
- **Falls feel right.** Get tackled and you stay down as long as you're stunned, then get up.
- **Keepers** step along their line, dive, catch and hold the ball with their own animations.
- **Match movement.** In a match you jog, backpedal and idle like a player, then go back to your normal walk when it ends.
- **The Infirmary** is furnished, Nurse Agatha Thorne is on duty, and there's often a patient resting on bed 1.
- **A new pace.** Everyone walks at 10 and sprints at 20, building to 27. Students and staff move by the same rules you do.
- **On the phone.** Students who take a call while walking now hold their phone.
- **For staff:** the Headmaster's Console (F3) replaces the old desk, with saved warnings, notes, bans and mutes, and a staff log. It helps the team keep the school fair.
