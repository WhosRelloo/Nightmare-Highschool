# Football tune-up (1.14.1)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-03**

**Power levels**
- QBs now set their throw power with **Z / C**, then **click** to throw. Low power lofts a soft lob over the defence, high power fires a flat bullet into a window. Your power shows above the controls: LOB, SPIRAL or BULLET.
- Passes spiral now, and bullets spin tighter.

**Catching takes skill**
- Your reach is smaller, the click window is shorter, and spamming locks your hands out longer. Get under the ball.
- **Dive and click** to lay out for a catch or a pick. A dive alone won't catch it.
- A faint ring on the grass shows where every ball in the air will come down.

**Fixes**
- Kickoffs can be caught, and you can juke and spin on a kick return.
