# Moon phases, the crimson moon and the blue moon (1.41.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-05**

- **The moon has phases now.** Full, waning, new and waxing, with a brand-new hand-made moon in the sky and its icon by your clock.
- **New moon:** the darkest night of the cycle. More stars, less light, and the Wraiths get stronger and harder to spot.
- **Full moon:** Werewolves' eyes now glow amber all night. Everyone will know.
- **The crimson moon:** a rare blood moon. Vampires hit harder and their eyes burn red; Ghouls and Wraiths stir too.
- **The blue moon:** a rare holy light. Priests and Humans stand stronger until dawn.
