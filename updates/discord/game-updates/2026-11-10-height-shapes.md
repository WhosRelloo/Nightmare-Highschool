📏 **Nightmare Highschool: Height that looks like height** (dev 1.70.0)

Tall students used to just be bigger copies. Now height changes your shape.

🦵 **Taller = longer**
Tall students get longer legs, arms and torso, with the legs growing most. They also get slightly thicker limbs, so a 7'0" student looks lanky, not inflated. Short students are compact the same way.

🧢 **Heads stay in proportion**
Heads only grow a little, so tall students don't have giant heads.

✅ **Nothing else changes**
Same heights, same speed, same reach. Clothes, hair, capes, weapons, sitting and every body type fit at every height.
