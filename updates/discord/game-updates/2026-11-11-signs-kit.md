# The signs kit (1.82.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-11**

- **The school is getting its signs.** Brass plaques on the classroom doors, carved stone tablets over them, enamel plates on the restroom doors, arrow boards, floor numbers, "Quiet Please" stands and a lit **WAY OUT** lantern over the exits.
- **Find your way between the wings.** Big wing directories hang from the corridor ceilings: the wing's name, then up to five places with an arrow each. Hanging corridor signs and signs that stick out from the wall read from both sides.
- **59 hand-drawn icons**, one for every class and room: a compass for Mathematics, a flask for Chemistry, an hourglass for History, an owl for Quiet Please, a coffin door for the Crypt, a planchette for the Séance room, and Boys, Girls and Accessible restrooms.
- All in the school's look: brass, black iron, limestone, crimson and ink-blue enamel, gilt lettering.
- **For the builder:** every sign is a blank you fill in, with any icon, any words and any enamel colour, so new rooms and wings get signs without waiting on new models.
