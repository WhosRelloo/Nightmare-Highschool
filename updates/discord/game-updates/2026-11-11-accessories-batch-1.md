# Accessories, part one: pins, sashes, ties and bags (1.79.0)

**Nightmare Highschool dev update, 2026-11-11**

- **Real 3D accessories are here.** The first set is all about school: crest pins for every clique, the Prefect's and Head Student's sashes with their medals, a school tie and a velvet bow tie, a leather satchel, a messenger bag, a strap of books, the Ink Club's chained grimoire (it glows faintly), a choir collar and a falling ruff.
- **They move with you.** Ties flutter, and the bags, books and grimoire swing as you walk and run.
- **They fit you.** Every piece sits right on every body type and height, and over your hairstyle. If a hairstyle would poke through a piece, the Wardrobe tells you, and changing to that hair takes the piece off.
- **Some things don't go together.** A sash and a bag strap would cross, so picking one takes the other off, and the Wardrobe tells you what came off.
- **Buy them** at the School Store's new Accessories tab, and **wear them** from a locker-room wardrobe. Clique pins and the grimoire are for members of that clique only.
- **Your sword comes first.** The satchel hangs on your right, away from the katana, and the book strap tucks away while your weapon is out. (The satchel and the Head Student's sash arrive shortly in their new versions.)
- Purely cosmetic: no stats.
- Coming next: hats and masks, then race-flavoured pieces, hunter gear, magic, and more.
