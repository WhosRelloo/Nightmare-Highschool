# 🌙 THE LIVING SCHOOL 🌙
**Nightmare Highschool, big update wave (1.10 to 1.11), 2026-11-02**

The halls aren't empty any more. Students walk in before the bell, take their seats when class starts, crowd the hallway between periods, queue at the new snack stand at lunch, and drift off to their clubs after school. The school runs on its own timetable now, whether you show up or not.

**🍱 Mrs. Okada's Concession Stand**
- A new snack kiosk sits by the west doors.
- Food heals you over a few seconds.
- Drinks give a small buff: a steadier guard, quieter footsteps, a sharper mind in class, or (if the teachers aren't looking) a Red Lantern.

**🎒 Your Locker** (press **`**)
- Your character, your gear and what it adds up to, every snack you carry, and a pouch that puts snacks on your hotbar.

**📚 Study Groups** (press **U**)
- Team up with up to three classmates. They show in the corner of your screen, and fighting side by side earns everyone more EXP.

**📜 The Report Card** (press **M**)
- Your level, your stats, everything your bloodline, clan, gear and snacks give you, and your school record, all on one sheet.

**🎬 Cinematic View** (press **\\**)
- Hide every bit of UI behind letterbox bars and take the perfect shot of the school at night.

**⌨️ New menu keys**
- Options **P** · Report Card **M** · Locker **`** · Study Group **U** · Factions **L** · Journal **J** · Students **Tab**
- They're all kept clear of combat and movement.

**Also in this wave**
- Gear chests stand up properly.
- Hiding in a locker shows the room through the vents.
- The barber and wardrobes frame you properly.
- Cameras can no longer get stuck.

See you in the halls. Class is never truly dismissed.
