# 🕯️ THE LIVING SCHOOL 🕯️
**Nightmare Highschool, big update wave (2.27 to 2.30), 2026-11-18**

The school doesn't wait for you anymore. Your classmates have names and long memories, the teachers keep the register, the halls are full of talk, and when the bell rings at night, somebody is listening.

**👥 Classmates With Names**
- 24 named students, three in each clique, each with a look and a temper of their own.
- Stop and look at one and they'll introduce themselves. Classmates you've met are listed under Tab.
- They remember you: shove one and you've made a grudge; share a snack and they'll thank you next time.

**📚 Teachers and Timetables**
- Ms. Sallow, Mr. Hale, Mr. Pryce and Dr. Whitlock teach at their desks, and the Headmaster keeps the school in line.
- Every class seats its own six classmates, and some of them skip.
- Start a fight in front of a teacher and they'll put their name on it.

**🗣️ Rumours and Scenes**
- What happens at school doesn't stay quiet. Students pass rumours on, and every retelling stretches the story, but never about you.
- Six small scenes play out around you: arguments, a locker shove, a defaced notice board, footsteps at dusk, a figure in the hall, a dare. Step in or walk on.

**📻 The PA and the Midnight Broadcast**
- The PA chimes and announces what's really happening: the bell, lunch, a substitute, a rumble.
- Night FM is remade as a full music app, with a gilt-and-lacquer look of its own.
- After 9 PM, tune in to the Midnight Broadcast. The host has a lot to say, and some of it is about Mira Holt.

**🌙 Curfew**
- Curfew starts at 10 PM. The hall monitor walks the halls until morning.
- Get caught in the building after hours and it goes on your record. The dorms are safe.

The school is awake. Mind what you say in the halls.
