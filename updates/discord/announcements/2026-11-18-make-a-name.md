# 📜 MAKE A NAME 📜
**Nightmare Highschool, big update wave (2.31 to 2.34), 2026-11-18**

Nightmare High keeps a file on every student, and now yours tells your story. What you do, where you go and who you beat all add up. The school notices, and so does everyone in the halls.

**🧭 Standing Paths**
- Five paths: Academic, Social, Combat, Athletic and Supernatural. Each has five tiers you climb by playing your way.
- Each tier earns a title. From tier 4 the PA or the Midnight Broadcast says your name.
- Classmates, leaders and teachers start greeting you by your reputation.

**🗂️ The Permanent Record (M)**
- M now opens your school file: a gilt-and-leather dossier with a cover for your race, animated and easy to read.
- Notable Entries keep a log of the moments that made your year.
- 30 Honours to earn, from "First Pass" to the hard ones you'll have to work for.

**🏆 Trophies**
- Nine modelled trophies: the Club Cup, the Honour Roll Plaque, the Ring Belt, the Register and five Path Medals.
- Watch them turn in the Trophy Case, and see them on the trophy wall in your dorm room.

**👔 Wear Your Reputation**
- Path tiers unlock things to wear: the Academic Pin, Scarred Knuckle Wraps, the Silver Sigil, and the Rolled Sleeves, Loosened Tie and Worn Blazer uniforms. None of it is sold.
- The Today card on your phone keeps it all in one quiet glance: what's on now, the latest rumour, your next step and your quest lead.

Your file is open. Make it worth reading.
