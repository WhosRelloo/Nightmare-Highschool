# 🕯️ BY CANDLELIGHT 🕯️
**Nightmare Highschool, big update wave (2.37 to 2.41), 2026-11-25**

The school got quieter, and it's watching closer. The screen steps back so you can see the halls, the sky is painted for every hour, and when staff catch you misbehaving, the Bells start to ring.

**🔔 The Bells**
- Misdeeds the staff see build Heat, and Heat rings up to five Bells, from Murmur to The Tolling.
- Ring enough and the hall monitors hunt you. You may be summoned to Room 106.
- Lie low and the Bells fall quiet again.

**🕸️ Quiet Corners**
- The HUD eases back when you're calm and steps up in a fight.
- Notifications are paper slips, and only the nearest prompt shows a key.
- The Journal is an open book, and case files are the Evidence Wall: pin clues and link them.

**🌒 Skies and Lighting**
- 19 painted skies across seven times of day, from Dawn to the Witching hour.
- Full, new and crimson moons each change the night, and the seasons bring their own skies.

**⚽ Soccer 2.0**
- New animation for every move: kicks meet the ball, dives and dribbles carry you, and keepers catch and hold.
- Tackles drop you for as long as you're stunned.

**🏥 The Infirmary and a New Pace**
- The Infirmary is furnished, with Nurse Agatha Thorne on duty.
- Everyone walks at 10 and sprints at 20, building to 27, and the students and staff move by the same rules you do.

**🕯️ Chat by Candlelight**
- The chat fades to just its lines when it's quiet and wakes when you press /.
- The Factions panel (L) is lit by a candle, with your rank shown as pips.
- The left-edge buttons are gone. Use L, J or your Phone.

The halls are dim. Keep your voice down.
