# 🩸 CHAPTER ONE: THE EMPTY DESK 🩸
**Nightmare Highschool, big update wave (2.22 to 2.26), 2026-11-18**

Every morning Mr. Hale reads the register. Every morning one name gets no answer. Chapter One is here: a story that starts on your first day in Room 102 and ends there, after dark, with you holding the file nobody was meant to find.

**🔔 The Roll Call**
- New students start Chapter One with a cutscene: the bell, the hurry to Room 102, and the register.
- Answer to your own name. Then listen for the one nobody answers.
- A short Orientation with Mr. Hale, and the empty desk in the back row.

**🎬 Cutscenes You Play**
- Cutscenes can stop and ask you to act: press on the beat, mash, dodge, hold, or answer in time.
- Alone, the scene follows you the instant you press. With your study group, it follows the group.
- Every interactive cutscene is graded S to D, and your best grade is kept in the Journal (J).

**✒️ The Strikethrough**
- After Hours ends in a fight in Room 102 at night.
- Dodge, shove, tug the file back, and finish it. Every miss changes the scene.
- Keep the file, or watch half of Mira's record go with it.

**🌙 Foundations**
- One school clock: lamps, wolves, beasts, the moon, hunger and the cold all keep the same hours.
- The Night Custodian sweeps the hall by day. Nobody has seen his face.
- Cutscenes you've seen can be watched again from the Journal, skips are a group vote, and a disconnect plays the scene again when you're back.

**Also in this wave**
- New Kid starts at the Enrollment desk, and your first quest waits until you walk into school.
- Texts looks like a messaging app, in the school's dress.
- Conversations hand the camera back cleanly.

Mira Holt was here. The register says otherwise.
