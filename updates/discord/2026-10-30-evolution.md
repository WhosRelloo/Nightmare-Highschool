## 🌕 Nightmare Highschool: Evolve Your Bloodline

**🩸 Hunger:** every race hungers for something. Vampires want blood, werewolves the hunt (and the full moon makes it worse), ghouls want to feed, wraiths want souls. Humans steel their resolve; priests keep the faith. Your Bloodline tab shows how close you are.

**🕯️ Rites:** when the hunger is full, a rite opens. Drink from a font beneath the Archive. Hunt under a full moon. Dig through the boiler ash. Slip past Mr. Hale to touch a cold mirror. Light the votives. Each race has its own, at night, at dawn, or in the ring.

**🎬 Evolve:** finish your rite and your bloodline turns, with a cutscene built for your race. Bats and crimson mist for vampires. A howl at the moon for werewolves. Halos and light for priests.

**🦷 The bite:** monsters who have evolved can **turn** a downed human or priest. The bitten get to choose: accept the bite, or fight it off.

*Earn your tier. Don't get bit. Or maybe you do.*
