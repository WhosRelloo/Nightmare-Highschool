# Tutorial animation chains: how each handoff is made

Generated by `build.py`. **Raw gap**: the body-pose distance (weighted joint angle in radians plus torso height,
heading and ground position left out) if A's last frame went straight into B's first. **Cut gap**: at the cut
build.py chose (pose and motion 3 frames either side). **Window**: the baked blend; *staged* means the torso leads
and the head comes last. **Re-seat**: the turn and ground shift baked into B's torso so the root never moves.

## Measured in Studio

`chaincheck.luau` played every chain on an R6 dummy at 60 fps (NH Combat Test, 2026-11-20); the full output is in
`review/chaincheck_result.txt`. Every runtime switch peaks below the chain's own p99 except the two entries into the
sneak loop (`Drainpipe1`, `HomeAgain1`), whose frames were checked offline and are identical to the loop on its own at
that phase (the peak is the stride). Baked blends: the "blend peak vs sources" figures printed by `build.py`, all within
1.18x or under 45 st/s².
